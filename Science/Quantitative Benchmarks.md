# Quantitative Benchmarks

Single source of truth for numbers cited from the science report. Do not invent figures beyond these sources.

## Carbon permanence (F_perm / % remaining)

| Source | Finding |
|---|---|
| [IPCC 2019 App. 4](https://www.ipcc-nggip.iges.or.jp/public/2019rf/pdf/4_Volume4/19R_V4_Ch02_Ap4_Biochar.pdf) | 100-yr F_perm: **0.65 / 0.80 / 0.89** (low/med/high T) |
| [Woolf et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.1c02425) | At 14.9 °C: 100-yr ≈ **0.63 / 0.71 / 0.82**; 1000-yr high-T ~0.25 |
| [Lehmann et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41561-021-00852-8) | H/C_org < 0.5 subset: mean **~82%** remaining at 100 yr (95% of cases >50%) |
| [EBC](https://www.european-biochar.org/) / [IBI](https://biochar-international.org/) | H/C_org ≤ **0.7**; O/C_org often ≤ **0.4** |

| Gas | Benchmark |
|---|---|
| N₂O | Historical metas ~−12% to −54% ([Cayuela 2014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agee.2013.10.009)); recent field ~**−23%** to **−26.8%** ([Jia 2023](https://doi.org/10.1002/ldr.4868)) |

## Carbon fraction (Woolf 2021 examples)

- Wood pyrolysis mean FC ≈ **0.76**; high-T wood ≈ **0.81** ([Woolf et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.1c02425))
- Manure ≈ **0.39**; sewage sludge ≈ **0.37**
- Gasification sludge/manure ~**0.10–0.14**

## Application rates

- Research: **~5–50+ t ha⁻¹** (sometimes 100; [Jeffery 2011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agee.2011.08.015) extremes)
- Nutrient-enriched fertilizers: **<2 t ha⁻¹**
- [Woolf 2021](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.1c02425) N₂O credit: only **>10 Mg C ha⁻¹** year 1
