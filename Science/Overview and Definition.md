# Overview and Definition

## Definition

**Biochar** is a solid, porous, carbon-rich material produced by pyrolysis of biomass under oxygen-free or oxygen-limited conditions.

**IUPAC (2024)** defines it as a solid multi-functional porous carbonaceous product of agricultural biomass pyrolysis in the temperature range **350–1000 °C**, with molar **H/C_org < 0.7** and **O/C_org < 0.4** to ensure abundant fused aromatic structures and distinguish it from inadequately carbonized materials (Bilias et al., 2024; DOI: [10.1515/pac-2021-0106](https://doi.org/10.1515/pac-2021-0106)).

**IPCC (2019)** inventory appendix defines biochar for GHG accounting as solid material from heating biomass **>350 °C** under controlled/limited oxidant (pyrolysis or gasification), applicable to mineral soils in cropland/grassland ([IPCC 2019 Refinement, Vol. 4, Appendix 4](https://www.ipcc-nggip.iges.or.jp/public/2019rf/pdf/4_Volume4/19R_V4_Ch02_Ap4_Biochar.pdf)).

## Not the same as

| Material | Distinction |
|---|---|
| Charcoal | Traditionally fuel / metallurgy |
| Activated carbon | Further activated for sorption |
| Hydrochar | Hydrothermal carbonization; typically higher H/C, less aromatic |
| Biocoal / torrefied biomass | Mainly energy fuel |
