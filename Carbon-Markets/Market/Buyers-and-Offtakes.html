3. Why these buyers choose BCR

Synthesized from public statements collected in Snapshot 2025 (not invented):

  1. Ready-now — shorter path from contract to delivery than many engineered peers (Market Volumes and Pricing).
  2. Cost-efficient relative to DAC/BECCS — qualitative consensus; exact premiums unpublished.
  3. Start-small optionality — trials under 100 t lower organizational barriers.
  4. MRV / standard trust — Puro CORCs, Isometric modules, CCP labels, dMRV (Carbonfuture).
  5. Co-benefits — soil, air quality, community, waste diversion (deal-specific).
  6. Permanence framing — ≥100y / several centuries / 1000y depending on pathway (Durable CDR Quality Tiers · Permanence Science for Credits).

Nasdaq additionally frames purchases as supporting marketplace scale and UN SDG co-benefits (Snapshot).

Microsoft–Exomad–Carbonfuture (Bolivia)

  • Dec 2023: >32,000 t via Carbonfuture MRV+; Puro Standard; delivery by Jun 2024 (Carbonfuture).
  • May 2025: ≥1.24 Mt / 10 years; Puro Biochar Methodology; Forest Monitoring Center + dMRV contractually embedded (Exomad).

Microsoft–Liferaft–Supercritical (US Midwest)

  • Mar 2026: 1 Mt CRUs / 10 years; Iowa & Illinois; compost-blended biochar; ICROA-endorsed registry (unnamed in release) (Liferaft).

Google biochar book

  • Jan 2025: 100 kt each from Varaha and Charm biochar by 2030 (Google).
  • Mar 2026: 200 ktCO₂e from AMP by 2030; MSW organics → biochar (AMP).

Altitude–Empacar (Bolivia CORCs)

  • Mar 2026: >305 kt CORCs; Puro or equivalent (Altitude).

Near-term inventory: Supercritical–Exomad

  • Apr 2026: Up to 500 kt over 3 years; remaining 2026 inventory + 2027–28; Puro (Supercritical).

High-durability niche: Microsoft–Carba–Isometric

  • Apr 2025: 44 kt / 5 years; landfill burial; Isometric 1,000-year durability (Carba).
