Who can list (supplier eligibility)

  • CO₂ Removal Supplier = biochar producer or contracted representative with sole CORC claim rights and end-to-end data access (rules 2.1.1–2.1.3)
  • Must comply with methodology + Puro Standard General Rules + local law
  • Path: Facility Audit (initial, typically on-site) → Certified Facility → Output Audits (at least annually) → CORC issuance

Additional operational feedstock rules

  • No co-firing with fossil coal/oil in feedstock
  • Impurity & fossil-C accounting (plastics, paints, sludge fossil C)
  • Stockpiling CH₄ controls
  • Biohazard treatment (≥500 °C × 3 min if relying on carbonization)

Cross-link: MRV Stack · Contaminants and Quality · Production

Production requirements

  • Thermochemical conversion: pyrolysis / gasification / modified combustion / other
  • H/C_org < 0.70 mandatory (rule 3.5.1) — hard gate for quantification eligibility
  • Reactor categorization: mode, process, heating, streams, co-product fate
  • Combustion of volatiles: design for complete combustion; ≥95% normal combustion operating time; flameout procedures; CH₄/N₂O monitoring rules
  • Fossil fuel use allowed for ignition/support if no fossil C enters biochar and LCA includes emissions (coal as process fuel not allowed)

Facility baselines under Edition 2025:

Baseline typeIntent

Retrofit / charcoal-repurpose baselines deduct prior char storage via Eqs. 6.6–6.9 (see quantification section below).

Mobile rules (Edition 2025): explicit Stationary vs Mobile facility definitions; spatial extent within one host country; fleet rules. Common US pitfall when units cross state lines that imply multi-country or unclear host-country scope — see Puro Project Cycle and MRV.

Retail Category R3 (v2, Nov 2025)

Eligible for CORCs only with:

  • Product-level particle size / moisture controls
  • Consumer labelling
  • Contractual clarity with last tracked intermediary
  • Country-level reversal-risk discount (RDF) on (C_{\mathrm{stored}})
  • Conservative transport accounting

Source: Dec 2025 retail update

Even with v2 safeguards, consumer channels remain higher-risk than contracted farm application (Risks and Controversies).

Additionality / baseline

Three components (rule 3.3.1):

  1. Carbon additionality
  2. Regulatory additionality
  3. Financial additionality

Per Puro Additionality Assessment Requirements + Baseline and Additionality Questionnaire (made public after successful Facility Audit).

Biochar-specific financial analysis must cover:

  • Biochar sales prices
  • Co-product revenues
  • Cost structure

Pitfall: waste-treatment cost-saving projects may fail financial additionality if savings alone make the project viable without CORCs (rule 3.3.4). See Puro Project Cycle and MRV · Risks and Controversies.

Lifecycle emissions, leakage, and net CORCs

Overall equation (5.2.1):

[ \mathrm{CORCs} = C_{\mathrm{stored}} - C_{\mathrm{baseline}} - C_{\mathrm{loss}} - E_{\mathrm{project}} - E_{\mathrm{leakage}} ]

TermMeaning
(C_{\mathrm{stored}})Stored biochar C as CO₂e: (Q_{\mathrm{biochar}} \times C_{\mathrm{org}} \times 44/12) (Eq. 6.1)
(C_{\mathrm{baseline}})Removals that would have occurred anyway (incl. retrofit / charcoal-repurpose deductions via Eqs. 6.6–6.9)
(C_{\mathrm{loss}})Expected decay losses via persistence fraction PF (Eq. 6.3 conceptually: (C_{\mathrm{stored}} \times (100 - PF)/100) with PF in %)
(E_{\mathrm{project}})Full LCA operational + embodied emissions; allocation to co-products; cut-offs
(E_{\mathrm{leakage}})Identify, mitigate, quantify residual market/activity-shifting leakage

Net CDR logic in words: Stored biochar C (as CO₂e) minus expected decay losses minus baseline removals that would have occurred anyway minus project LCA emissions minus unmitigated leakage — only for biochar proven in an eligible end use.

Permanence & quantification (Edition 2025)

Persistence fraction

[ PF = M - a \times (H/C_{\mathrm{org}}) \quad (\mathrm{Eq.\ 6.4}) ]

(M) and (a) from Table 6.1 by mean annual soil temperature (T_s) (°C). Examples from the deep dive:

(T_s)(M)(a)
7 °C (floor)96.5911.28
15 °C91.8728.16
20 °C89.8735.29
30 °C87.6643.10
40 °C86.1948.25

Model design choices:

  • Soil T from Puro-provided dataset (SBIO1 annual mean 5–15 cm); minimum 7 °C
  • 200-year power-law extrapolation; thereafter deeper-soil protection assumed → CORC200+
  • Uses lower bound of 80% CI (program-level conservatism)
  • H/C_org domain 0–0.7
  • Open source: https://github.com/puro-earth/PuroBiocharPersistenceEdition2025 (built on SLU biocharStability)

Worked intuition (from deep dive):

Soil TH/C_orgApprox PF
20 °C0.4(89.87 - 35.29 \times 0.4) ≈ 75.8%
20 °C0.2≈ 82.8%

Puro’s permanence stance vs millennial claims

Puro explicitly stopped at centuries, not millennia, pending integration of inertinite/Ro science (permanence post). Ro is encouraged for reporting but does not change CORC quantification under Edition 2025. Contrast with Isometric Biochar Protocol (200y or Ro≥2% → 1000y) and CSI Global Biochar C-Sink (upper GPC path via Ro≥3.8% or HyPy≥90%). Concept note: Puro CORC200+.

Reversal policy

  • No default buffer deduction (rule 4.2.1)
  • Risks managed via use eligibility + evidence
  • Supplier liable for reversals caused by non-compliance

Critics may prefer pooled buffers — see Risks and Controversies · Puro vs Other Standards.

ICVCM status for this methodology

As of ICVCM announcement 2025-12-11 and research date 2026-09-14:

  • Puro program is CCP-Eligible (General Rules v4.2+)
  • Biochar methodology is not yet CCP-Approved

Buyers wanting CCP-labelled credits today may prefer Verra VM0044 until Puro’s category assessment completes. Re-check ICVCM Assessment Status before relying on this. See Puro.earth Overview · Puro vs Other Standards.

When this methodology is the right fit

Prefer Edition 2025 / CORC200+ when you want:

  • Mature BCR buyer recognition + CORCCHAR price signal + Nasdaq registry infrastructure
  • Flexible baselines (New / Retrofit / Charcoal Repurpose) and explicit mobile fleet rules
  • A centuries-scale (200+) permanence claim with open-source PF model

Consider alternatives when you need CCP methodology labels today (Verra VM0044), a 1000-year Ro path (Isometric Biochar Protocol), or EBC/WBC-native GPC/SPC (CSI Global Biochar C-Sink). Full chooser: Puro vs Other Standards.
