# Liquid Nutrient Enrichment Field Trials

**Citation:** Schmidt H-P, Pandit BH, Cornelissen G, Kammann CI (2017). Biochar-based fertilization with liquid nutrient enrichment: 21 field trials covering 13 crop species in Nepal. *Land Degradation & Development* 28(8): 2324–2342. https://doi.org/10.1002/ldr.2761

**What it is:** A program of 21 on-farm trials in Nepal. Biochar from flame-curtain kilns was loaded with cow urine or dissolved NPK and placed in the root zone at low doses. Controls got the same nutrients without biochar, so the trials isolate biochar's role as a nutrient carrier.

## Use with care

- The large gains come from **enriched biochar placed in the root zone at ≤2 t ha⁻¹**, not from bulk raw char. Don't transfer them to broadcast applications.
- The +123% comparison is against local organic practice, not mineral NPK, and its spread (±76.7%) is wide.
- The full text wasn't accessible for this check. Trial-level details (crop list, sites, enrichment timing) still need confirming against the paper.
