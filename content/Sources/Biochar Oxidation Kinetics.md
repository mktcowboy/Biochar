# Biochar Oxidation Kinetics

**Citation:** Zimmerman AR (2010). Abiotic and Microbial Oxidation of Laboratory-Produced Black Carbon (Biochar). *Environ. Sci. Technol.* 44:1295–1301. https://doi.org/10.1021/es903140c

**What it does:** Measures abiotic and microbial oxidation of laboratory biochars and shows that more labile / less condensed chars mineralize faster—useful when linking low pyrolysis temperature or high volatile matter to short-term C loss.
