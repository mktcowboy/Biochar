# Ross & Emery 2025

**Citation:** Ross AT, Emery SM (2025). Microbial inocula enhance effects of biochar amendments on crop productivity, soil health, and microbial communities: A meta-analysis. *Soil Science Society of America Journal* 89(1): e20792 (online 5 Dec 2024). https://doi.org/10.1002/saj2.20792

**What it is:** The meta-analysis behind the vault's claim that adding microbial inocula to biochar helps on average, but mainly with local or research-grade inocula and mainly in greenhouse studies.

## Use with care

- The effect sizes are in the full paper, which wasn't re-checked. Quote directions of effect from this note, not magnitudes.
- The first author's surname is **Ross** (given names Aysha Tapp); some vault notes cite "Tapp Ross".
