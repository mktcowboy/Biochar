# Pacific Biochar Compost White Paper 2023

**Citation:** McIntosh C, Hunt J (2023). *Biochar + Compost*. Pacific Biochar Benefit Corporation, January 2023. https://pacificbiochar.com/wp-content/uploads/Pacific-Biochar_Biochar-Compost_white-paper.pdf

**What it is:** A practitioner synthesis from a biochar supplier on co-composting biochar and blending it with compost. It mixes the authors' commercial experience with citations to peer-reviewed studies.

## Use with care

- **Not peer reviewed**, and written by a biochar seller. The emission reductions are best cases from cited studies, so check the primary paper before quoting one as typical.
- It restates [[Co-compost Nitrate Capture|shows co-compost nitrate capture]] as a "yield increase of 305%". That paper's results put biomass at up to **305% *of* the control** (about 3×).
