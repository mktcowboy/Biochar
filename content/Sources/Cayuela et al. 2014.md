# Cayuela et al. 2014

**Citation:** Cayuela ML, van Zwieten L, Singh BP, Jeffery S, Roig A, Sánchez-Monedero MA (2014). Biochar's role in mitigating soil nitrous oxide emissions: A review and meta-analysis. *Agriculture, Ecosystems & Environment* 191: 5–16. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agee.2013.10.009

**What it is:** The classic review and meta-analysis of biochar and soil N₂O, and the source of the vault's "−54%" figure.

## Use with care

- The −54% mixes lab and field studies from an early literature base. The larger later synthesis [[Jia et al. 2023]] reports −26.8%.
- The finding that the char's **H:C_org** ratio moderates N₂O comes from a *separate* paper, Cayuela et al. 2015 (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agee.2014.12.015), not this one.
