# Kammann et al. 2015

**Citation:** Kammann CI, Schmidt H-P, Messerschmidt N, Linsel S, Steffens D, Müller C, Koyro H-W, Conte P, Joseph S (2015). Plant growth improvement mediated by nitrate capture in co-composted biochar. *Scientific Reports* 5: 11080. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep11080 (open access)

**What it is:** A greenhouse pot study with quinoa that compares untreated biochar with the same biochar after co-composting. It's the vault's clearest controlled contrast between "hungry" raw char and charged char.

## Use with care

- **Correction to earlier vault notes:** "+305%" overstates the result. Co-composted biochar biomass reached up to **305% *of* the control**, about 3× (roughly +205%). Untreated biochar reached 60% of the control.
- The vault's "~3–5 g NO₃⁻-N kg⁻¹" range isn't this paper's number. It reports ~2.1–2.2 g kg⁻¹ by the standard method and ~5.2 g kg⁻¹ by exhaustive washing.
- A pot study in one soil at 2% w/w: a demonstration of mechanism, not a field yield estimate.
