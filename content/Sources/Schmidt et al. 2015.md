# Schmidt et al. 2015

**Citation:** Schmidt H-P, Pandit BH, Martinsen V, Cornelissen G, Conte P, Kammann CI (2015). Fourfold increase in pumpkin yield in response to low-dosage root zone application of urine-enhanced biochar to a fertile tropical soil. *Agriculture* 5(3): 723–741. https://doi.org/10.3390/agriculture5030723 (open access)

**What it is:** A single pumpkin field trial in Nepal. It compared compost plus urine, compost plus biochar, and compost plus urine-loaded biochar, all placed in the root zone. It's the vault's reference for low-dose, urine-charged biochar.

## Use with care

- One site, one crop, one season: a strong demonstration, not an average effect. The multi-site follow-up is [[Schmidt et al. 2017]].
- The authors *tentatively* attribute the gain to an organic coating that urine loading forms on pore surfaces. The abstract offers this as an explanation, not a measured cause.
