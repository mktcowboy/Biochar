# ANSI ASABE USBI S668

**Citation:** ANSI/ASABE/USBI S668 NOV2025ED. *Methods for Measurement and Testing of Biochar*. Published by ASABE November 2025. PDF via American Biochar Institute: https://biochar.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ANSI-S668.pdf · document page: https://biochar.org/document/ansi-s668/

**What it is:** American National Standard listing **recommended test methods** for biochar materials (sampling, proximate/ultimate analysis, calorimetry, permanence assessment methods, physical properties, surface area/porosity, environmental contaminants including PAHs/PCBs/dioxins/PFAS/heavy metals, and agricultural parameters such as nutrients, pH, liming potential, and salinity). Front matter frames ASABE standards as informational/advisory and voluntary.
