# ABI 2025 US Biochar Market Report

**Citation:** American Biochar Institute (2025). *2025 United States Biochar Market Report*. https://biochar.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-United-States-Biochar-Market-Report.pdf · landing page: https://biochar.org/document/2025-united-states-biochar-market-report/

**What it is:** First ABI report focused specifically on benchmarking the **United States** biochar industry (follow-up to the 2023 Global Biochar Market Report). Based on an anonymous web survey of industry stakeholders; ABI states it was completed in fall 2025 in collaboration with the International Biochar Initiative and the State of Carbon Dioxide Removal.

## Use with care

- Survey of industry respondents — **not** the same as CDR.fyi credit **deliveries** or registry issuances ([[Market Volumes and Pricing]]).
- Carbon-revenue table footnotes: some production totals include **biocarbon**; do not equate table tonnes to “pure biochar only” without reading the note in the PDF.
