# Jeffery et al. 2011

**Citation:** Jeffery S, Verheijen FGA, van der Velde M, Bastos AC (2011). A quantitative review of the effects of biochar application to soils on crop productivity using meta-analysis. *Agriculture, Ecosystems & Environment* 144(1): 175–187. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agee.2011.08.015

**What it is:** The first widely cited meta-analysis of biochar effects on crop productivity (yield or above-ground biomass). Its subgroup results are where the vault's liming and water-holding explanations come from.

## Use with care

- The authors read the pH and texture subgroups as pointing to **liming, better water holding, and nutrient availability**. That's an interpretation of subgroup means, not a tested mechanism.
- The 100 t ha⁻¹ result is a research extreme, not a farm rate.
- Every underlying study ran only 1–2 years, so this says nothing about long-term yield effects.
