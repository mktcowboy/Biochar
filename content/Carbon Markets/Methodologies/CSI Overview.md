# CSI Overview

**Carbon Standards International AG (CSI)** is the Swiss standard-setter behind the **Global C-Sink** family and the **EBC / WBC** product certificates. For biochar CDR diligence, treat CSI as a **product + C-sink stack**, not a Nasdaq-style engineered-CDR marketplace like [[Puro.earth Overview]].

Hub method note: [[CSI Global Biochar C-Sink]]. Research: `/workspace/biochar-research/csi-c-sink-deep-dive.md` (2026-09-16).

## Global C-Sink Registry

All certified biochar C-sinks under the Global Biochar C-Sink standard are registered in the **[Global C-Sink Registry](https://global-c-registry.org/)**, owned/run by Carbon Standards ([v3.3 §1.2](https://www.carbon-standards.com/docs/transfer/4000039EN.pdf)).

Registry holds (per standard): application amount/location, biochar quality, persistence, year of original CO₂ removal, C-sink owner, and the **emission portfolio** that must be offset before the sink is usable for compensation.

- Public consultation of projects appears as status **`Ongoing Validation`** ([service page](https://www.carbon-standards.com/en/standards/service-501~global-biochar-c-sink.html))
- Transfers and retirements: endorsed **C-Sink Traders** only (same page)

## Global Artisan C-Sink (distributed track)

Separate from the industrial EBC→C-Sink path. Key eligibility claims from CSI ([service-505](https://www.carbon-standards.com/en/standards/service-505~global-artisan-c-sink.html); [v2.1A PDF](https://www.carbon-standards.com/docs/transfer/4000136EN.pdf)):

| Rule | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Geography | World Bank **low / lower-middle / higher-middle** income countries |
| Technology | Artisan / Kon-Tiki-type low-tech pyrolysis |
| Volume caps | Farmer ≤ **100 m³/year**; Artisan Pro ≤ **1500 m³/year** |
| Other | Fair compensation / social impact; not dual-certified under other nature-based climate methodologies for the same biomass/SOC story |

Process: CSI endorsement of Artisan C-Sink Manager → VVB validation (PDD) → VVB verification (incl. on-site) → registry. Endorsed dMRV providers and labs listed on the service page.

## EU CRCF path (CS-BCR)

In addition to Global Biochar C-Sink, CSI publishes **CS-BCR** (*EU Biochar Carbon Removal Standard*) for activities as **permanent carbon removals** under Regulation **(EU) 2024/3012** and Commission Delegated Regulation **(EU) 2026/285** ([service page](https://www.carbon-standards.com/en/standards/service-501~global-biochar-c-sink.html); [CS-BCR 0.7 PDF](https://www.carbon-standards.com/docs/transfer/4000289EN.pdf); [EUR-Lex 2026/285](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32026R0285)).

CSI states CS-BCR is **self-contained**: once the Commission approves recognition, compliance with CS-BCR alone is intended to demonstrate Delegated Regulation compliance — aiming for credits comparable to other CRCF units. **Public consultation: 26 Aug 2026 – 24 Sep 2026** (service page as of 2026-09-16 research).

Do not conflate live **Global Biochar C-Sink 3.3** issuance with unfinished CS-BCR recognition.
