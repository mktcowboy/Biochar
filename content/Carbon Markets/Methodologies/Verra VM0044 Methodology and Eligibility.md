## Facility newness — greenfield gate

VM0044 v1.1 states the project must install and operate a **new (greenfield)** biochar production facility(ies) ([v1.1 PDF](https://verra.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/VM0044-Methodology-for-Biochar-Utilization-in-Soil-and-Non-Soil-Applications-v1.1.pdf)).

| Implication | Detail |
|---|---|
| Existing / retrofit plants | **Out of scope** under current active framing until **v2.0** expands eligibility |
| v2.0 draft | Explicitly proposes expansion to **existing** facilities ([consultation](https://verra.org/consultation-major-revision-to-biochar-methodology-vm0044/)) |
| Contrast Puro | Puro Edition 2025 allows **New / Retrofit / Charcoal Repurpose** baselines — see [[Puro Biochar Methodology Edition 2025]] |
| Contrast Isometric | Project-specific; not framed as Verra-style greenfield lock — [[Isometric Biochar Protocol]] |

For US producers with an already-operating kiln: Verra v1.2 may be a **non-starter** until v2.0 is approved and adopted — watch the consultation outcome.

## Thermochemical scope

| Eligible | Excluded | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Pyrolysis, gasification, biomass boilers (terms used interchangeably for this methodology) | **Torrefaction** and **hydrothermal carbonization** | v1.1 §4 — excluded because solids are not significantly more persistent than feedstock ([Woolf et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.1c02425)) |

Health & safety program protecting workers from airborne pollutants is required (v1.1 applicability).

## High-tech vs low-tech (four-condition gate)

**High technology** facilities must meet **all** of the following (v1.1 definitions, adapted from EBC Production Guidelines):

| # | Condition |
|---|---|
| **(a)** | Pyrolytic GHGs **recovered or combusted** — not allowed to escape to atmosphere |
| **(b)** | ≥ **70%** of pyrolysis heat energy **used** (accounting for heat-transfer inefficiencies) |
| **(c)** | Pollution controls (e.g., thermal oxidizer) meeting local / national / international emission thresholds (strictest where overlapping) |
| **(d)** | Production **temperature measured and reported** |

Fail any condition → **low technology** production facility (different quantification / monitoring defaults). See [[Verra VM0044 Permanence and Quantification]] and [[Verra VM0044 Project Cycle and MRV]].

**Non-soil applications are restricted to high-tech biochar only** (v1.1 applicability condition 11).

v2.0 consultation flags “detailed criteria for high-technology biochar production facilities” as a revision theme ([consultation](https://verra.org/consultation-major-revision-to-biochar-methodology-vm0044/)).

## Feedstock — waste biomass only

### Core tests (must all hold)

From v1.1 §4:

1. Purely **biogenic waste biomass** — **not purpose-grown**
2. Otherwise **left to decay** or **combusted for purposes other than energy production** (Appendix 2 demonstration procedures)
3. **Not imported from other countries**
4. Meets **Table 1** sustainability criteria for its sourcing category

### Table 1 categories (non-exhaustive examples)

| Category | Example themes (v1.1 Table 1) | Notable safeguards |
|---|---|---|
| Agricultural waste biomass | Prunings; straw/leaves/stalks/husk/pomace; fruit/veg residues | Field residues: document no decline in soil C / productivity **or** baseline open burning; else remove ≤ **50%** of residues |
| Food processing residues | Wash/peel/centrifuge residues; expired food; coffee/tea/cocoa/tobacco processing | Residue production per output must not increase to chase biochar |
| Forestry & wood processing | Off-cuts, sawdust, mill by-products; sustainable forest sources | Forest-sourced wood must not drive deforestation/degradation |
| Recycling economy | Urban/rural green waste; biosolids; paper mill sludge | Contaminant thresholds per IBI/EBC |
| Aquaculture plants | Seaweed/algae waste; aquatic invasive (Plantae) | No purposeful introduction of invasives for feedstock |
| Animal manure | Swine, cattle, horse, poultry wastes | By-products of husbandry |
| High-carbon fly ash (HCFA) | Bioenergy cogeneration by-product | ≤ **5%** of annual waste biomass throughput; affirmative extraction tech; no fossil makeup for diverted calories; meet IBI |

**Mixed feedstocks** must still meet IBI Biochar Testing Guidelines or EBC Production Guidelines (v1.1).

### Transport of feedstock / biochar

- Non-road (ships/boats/other) allowed up to **200 km**
- Distances **> 200 km** must use road vehicles per **CDM Tool 12** framing (v1.1)

### Mineral additives

Lime, rock minerals, ash ≤ **10%** dry mass without extra proof; above 10% requires lab evidence against IBI/EBC contaminant thresholds (v1.1).

### Appendix 2 — waste demonstration

Credible evidence of baseline fate (decay / non-energy combustion): government records, disposal facility records, production records; else literature / surveys of similar industries (v1.1 §6 / App. 2 themes). This is a frequent project failure mode called out by practitioner checklists — treat as **validation-critical**, not deferrable.

v2.0 proposes expanding eligible feedstocks (invasive species; other new non-purpose-grown streams) ([consultation](https://verra.org/consultation-major-revision-to-biochar-methodology-vm0044/)).

## End-use eligibility

### Timing

Biochar must be utilized in soil or non-soil applications within **one year** of production (v1.1 condition 9) — decay math is calibrated to 100-year permanence from application, not from multi-year stockpiles.

### Soil applications

| Rule | Detail (v1.1) |
|---|---|
| Eligible land | Cropland, grassland, vegetated urban soils, forest — **not wetlands** |
| Placement | Surface or subsurface |
| Surface rule | Must mix with compost, manure, or anaerobic digestate |
| Subsurface | May be pure or mixed |
| Contaminants | IBI Testing Guidelines or EBC Production Guidelines (or relevant national regs) |
| Chemistry gate | Molar **H:Corg ≤ 0.7** |

Contrast Isometric soil module: **H/Corg < 0.5** and **O/Corg < 0.2** ([soil module](https://registry.isometric.com/module/biochar-storage-soil-environments/1.3)) — tighter aromaticity gate. See [[Isometric Modules Soil Built Burial]].

### Non-soil applications

| Rule | Detail (v1.1) |
|---|---|
| Examples | Cement, asphalt, other long-lived products |
| Tech restriction | **High-tech only** |
| Carbon content | Biochar ≥ **50% C** dry weight |
| Longevity proof | Lab results, peer-reviewed papers, or third-party product assessment including lifetime |
| End-of-life | Must demonstrate biochar in the product is **not combusted** at end of life |

Isometric’s dedicated [[Isometric Modules Soil Built Burial|built-environment module]] adds Ro-based 1000y math and demolition/abrasion reversal modeling — different architecture than Verra’s non-soil PR_de path.

| Version | Approach | Source |

**Practical:** v1.2 projects must budget for investment analysis documentation, not only the activity-penetration / positive-list narrative.

## Baseline framing

Baseline: continuation of pre-project waste handling where waste biomass would be left to decay or combusted for non-energy purposes (v1.1). Default net baseline emission avoidance at sourcing stage treated conservatively as **zero** in the methodology’s BESS framing (v1.1 §8.1.1) — removal claim centers on durable biochar C, not methane avoidance stacking.

## US / ag design notes (no invented Prairie facts)

| Design choice | VM0044 implication |
|---|---|
| New kiln vs retrofit | Greenfield required under current active rules |
| Ag residues from fields | Soil-C / productivity documentation or ≤50% residue removal |
| Compost / manure “matrix” pathways | Aligns with surface-application mixing rule |
| Concrete / asphalt offtake | High-tech + ≥50% C + longevity evidence |
| Want existing facility credited | Watch **v2.0**; consider [[Puro Biochar Methodology Edition 2025]] or [[Isometric Biochar Protocol]] in parallel diligence |
