## Why comparison matters

Biochar carbon removal (BCR) buyers often diligence **MRV stringency, permanence claim, and integrity labels** more than brand alone. The same physical tonne generally **cannot** be credited under two registries ([[Double Counting and Credit Ownership]]). Choosing a pathway early locks feedstock rules, end-use evidence, and offtake narratives for years — especially under Puro’s Edition 2022 → 2025 transition through **2030**.

## Permanence science split (the core intellectual difference)

Two camps appear across standards:

| Camp | Programs / claims | Inputs |
|---|---|---|
| **Decay-model / centuries** | Puro **CORC200+**; Isometric **200y** path; IPCC / Woolf-style framing | H/C_org, soil T, multi-pool or power-law decay, confidence intervals |
| **Inertinite / Ro millennial** | Isometric **1000y** Ro path; CSI upper **GPC**; Sanei et al. science | Random reflectance, HyPy, inertinite benchmarks |

**Puro’s explicit stance:** stopped at **centuries**, not millennia, pending integration of Ro/inertinite science; Ro reporting **encouraged** but **does not yet change quantification** ([permanence post](https://puro.earth/insights/post/biochar-permanence/)).

Deep science: [[Permanence]] · [[Permanence Science for Credits]] · [[Biochar Soil GHG Inventory Model|models soil GHG from biochar]] ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.1c02425)) · [[Inertinite Permanence Benchmark|inertinite permanence benchmark]] ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.coal.2023.104409)) · Azzi et al. 2024 ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geoderma.2023.116761))

## Facility / feedstock / issuance differences that change project design

### Facility baselines

| Feature | Puro Ed. 2025 | Verra VM0044 (per deep dive) |
|---|---|---|
| New facilities | Yes | Yes (emphasized) |
| Retrofit | Yes | Not the Puro-style triad |
| Charcoal repurpose | Yes | — |
| Mobile fleets | Explicit Stationary vs Mobile; one host country | Project-specific under VCS; not Puro’s fleet framing |

### Issuance trigger

| Program | Trigger |
|---|---|
| **Puro** | First **durable eligible use** + Output Audit (ex-post use) |
| **Verra** | VCS validation/verification cycle |
| **Isometric** | Ex-post after storage |
| **CSI** | C-sink after emission-portfolio offsetting |

Puro’s use-proof requirement is a major ops difference for US producers with diffuse retail channels — see [[Puro Project Cycle and MRV]] and R3 v2 in [[Puro Biochar Methodology Edition 2025]].

### Buffer philosophy

Puro: **no default buffer** % — eligibility + evidence + supplier liability.  
Critics may prefer pooled buffers; defenders argue ex-ante eligibility is tighter. Watch-list: [[Risks and Controversies]].

## Practical chooser (US producer)

| Choose **Puro** when… | Choose **Verra VM0044** when… | Choose **Isometric** when… | Choose **CSI** when… |
|---|---|---|---|
| You want mature BCR buyer recognition + **CORCCHAR** price signal + Nasdaq registry infrastructure | You need a **CCP-labelled methodology today** + VCS buyer familiarity; project is clearly a **new** waste facility under Appendix 2 | Buyer demands **1000-year Ro path** or maximal soil MRV stringency; willing to pay for high-integrity niche pricing | You already hold **EBC/WBC** and want GPC/SPC + EU **CS-BCR**/CRCF path ([[CSI Overview]]) |
| Mobile fleets / retrofit / charcoal-repurpose fit Puro baselines | Clean new + waste feedstock story | Strong lab/petrography capability for Ro (≥**2%**) | Artisan/small-scale Global Artisan C-Sink track; or industrial upper class at Ro≥**3.8%** / HyPy≥**90%** ([[CSI GPC vs SPC Permanence Classes]]) |
| You can deliver **eligible end-use evidence** (farm contracts preferred over diffuse retail) | Buyers require VCU plumbing | Offtake term sheet specifies Isometric | Product certification already in EBC/WBC stack ([[Product Standards EBC IBI]]) |

**Many large buyers are registry-agnostic on BCR but diligence MRV**; dual pathways are uncommon for the same tonne (double-counting rules). See [[Buyers and Offtakes]] · [[Double Counting and Credit Ownership]].

## Critique watch-list (cross-standard)

1. **Permanence science divergence** — centuries vs millennia ([[Permanence Science for Credits]])
2. **Puro ICVCM methodology lag** vs Verra CCP method approval
3. **Edition 2022 → 2025 transition** through **12 Jun 2030**; Preliminary Assessments obsolete
4. **Retail / diffuse application** reversal risk even after R3 v2
5. **No default buffer** vs pooled-buffer expectations
6. **Geographic / eligibility quirks** — mobile one-host-country; soil T 7 °C floor; sludge fossil-C burden; category P no CORCs; R3 country RDF
7. **Concentration / delivery risk** — BCR buyer and supplier concentration (CDR.fyi); contracted vs delivered gaps
8. **NGO scrutiny** of CDR frameworks generally (Fern, Carbon Market Watch on CRCF etc.) — apply carefully to biochar certification narratives

## Quick decision tree (non-advice)

1. Does the offtake **require CCP methodology labels today?** → lean [[Verra VM0044]] until Puro biochar is CCP-Approved.  
2. Does the offtake **require 1000-year Ro?** → lean [[Isometric Biochar Protocol]].  
3. Already **EBC/WBC** with EU CRCF / GPC goals? → evaluate [[CSI Global Biochar C-Sink]] · [[CSI Overview]].  
4. Need **public price signal**, retrofit/mobile baselines, and mature BCR recognition with **200+** claim? → [[Puro CORC200+]] / Edition 2025.  
5. Regardless: lock **end-use CoC** and **no double counting** before signing ([[Puro Project Cycle and MRV]] · [[Double Counting and Credit Ownership]]).
