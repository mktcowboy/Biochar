# Verra VM0044

**Full name:** *Biochar Utilization in Soil and Non-Soil Applications*  
**Operator:** Verra (Verified Carbon Standard)  
**Current version:** **v1.2**, active since **27 June 2025** ([methodology page](https://verra.org/methodologies/vm0044-biochar-utilization-in-soil-and-non-soil-applications-v1-2/))

- [Methodology page](https://verra.org/methodologies/vm0044-biochar-utilization-in-soil-and-non-soil-applications-v1-2/)
- [Revision announcement](https://verra.org/verra-publishes-minor-revision-to-biochar-methodology/)

## What it credits

CO₂ **removals** from converting **waste biomass** into biochar at **new** production facilities, used in eligible soil applications (crop/grassland) or durable non-soil applications (e.g., biochar-amended concrete). Global applicability. Sectoral scope: waste handling & disposal.

## Key MRV themes

- Feedstock demonstrated as **waste** (Appendix 2)
- Pyrolysis/gasification; high-tech vs low-tech pathways
- Biochar characterization including **H/C_org**; permanence factor lookup (Table 3) over **100-year** horizon; H/C_org ≥ ~0.7 typically excluded from high-tech path (confirm exact Table 3 in official PDF)
- Application / utilization evidence
- Third-party VCS validation/verification

## Additionality & permanence

- v1.2 **requires investment analysis (step 3)** under VT0008—added to align with ICVCM Core Carbon Principles ([revision announcement](https://verra.org/verra-publishes-minor-revision-to-biochar-methodology/))
- Crediting calibrated to **100-year** stability factors from H/C_org—not millennial inertinite crediting
