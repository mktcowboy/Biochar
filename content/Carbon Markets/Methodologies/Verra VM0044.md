## Why it matters for biochar

VM0044 is the main **VCS** pathway to issue **removal VCUs** from waste biomass → biochar → eligible soil or durable non-soil use. As of mid-2025 it is also one of the few biochar methodologies with an explicit **ICVCM CCP-Approved** category decision ([ICVCM M43 PDF](https://icvcm.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/M43_VCS_VM0044_v1.2_2025.pdf); [Verra announcement](https://verra.org/verras-biochar-and-forest-management-approaches-endorsed-by-icvcm-as-high-integrity-solutions/)).

For a US / Prairie-leaning producer comparing registries: start here for **CCP-labelled VCU** demand, then compare permanence horizons and facility rules against [[Puro Biochar Methodology Edition 2025]] and [[Isometric Permanence Pathways 200y and 1000y]].

## What it credits (one paragraph)

CO₂ **removals** from converting **waste biomass** into biochar at **new (greenfield)** production facilities, then using that biochar in eligible **soil** applications (e.g., crop and grasslands) or durable **non-soil** applications (e.g., biochar-amended concrete and building materials) ([methodology page](https://verra.org/methodologies/vm0044-biochar-utilization-in-soil-and-non-soil-applications-v1-2/)).
