## Why it matters for biochar

CSI sits at the intersection of **product certification (EBC/WBC)** and **C-sink accounting**. It does not issue a classic multi-sector offset unit; it certifies tracked **biochar C-sinks** split into:

| Pool | Meaning (v3.3) | Typical climate use |
|---|---|---|
| **GPC** | Geologically persistent carbon (**>1000 years**) | CO₂ / N₂O offsetting under CSI logic |
| **SPC** | Semi-persistent carbon (decays over **≤1000 years**) | Temporary climate services; CH₄ offsetting via TCE |

That GPC/SPC split — plus an **upper persistence class** unlocked by **Ro ≥ 3.8%** or **BC_HyPy ≥ 90%** — is CSI’s distinctive permanence story relative to Puro’s CORC200+ centuries claim and Verra’s 100-year Table 3 factors. See [[CSI GPC vs SPC Permanence Classes]] · [[Permanence Science for Credits]].

## Prerequisite: EBC / WBC

Only **EBC- or WBC-certified** biochar may receive Global Biochar C-Sink certification. Product class must match use (e.g. EBC-Agro / WBC-Agro for agricultural soil). Persistence analytics beyond the EBC Basic package (**Ro**, **HyPy**) must be requested **as part of the same EBC/WBC batch analysis** — separate sampling is not accepted ([v3.3 §1.3](https://www.carbon-standards.com/docs/transfer/4000039EN.pdf)).

Deepen: [[Product Standards EBC IBI]] · [EBC Standard 10.5E PDF](https://www.carbon-standards.com/docs/transfer/4000093EN.pdf) · [EBC service](https://www.carbon-standards.com/en/standards/service-492~production-of-biochar.html) · [WBC service](https://www.carbon-standards.com/en/standards/service-514~production-of-biochar.html)

## Persistence classes (v3.3 snapshot)

| Condition | Split |
|---|---|
| H/C_org **> 0.40** | **100% SPC**, 0% GPC |
| H/C_org **≤ 0.40** (lower class) | **75% GPC / 25% SPC** |
| Upper class: mean **Ro ≥ 3.8%** (distributional guards) **or** BC_HyPy **≥ 90%** | **90% GPC / 10% SPC** |

Full criteria, example Table 2, SPC decay formula, and materials rules: **[[CSI GPC vs SPC Permanence Classes]]**.

## Emission portfolio (snapshot)

All associated GHG from biomass → application must be registered and **offset before** C-sink validation ([v3.3 Ch. 4](https://www.carbon-standards.com/docs/transfer/4000039EN.pdf)):

- Fossil **CO₂** / **N₂O** → offset with **GPC**
- **CH₄** → temporary sinks delivering equal **TCE** within **5–20 years**
- Margin of safety **20 kg CO₂e / t biochar** (DM), factory gate
- Energy-use efficiency **>60%**; fossil process emissions plan toward **<100 kg CO₂e / t biochar by 2030** and **<20 kg by 2035** (exec summary wording — see MRV note for §4.5 “biochar-carbon” variant)

Detail: **[[CSI MRV and Emission Portfolio]]**.

## When to prefer CSI (compressed)

Prefer CSI when EBC/WBC is already in-stack, GPC/SPC + millennial GPC narrative (or Artisan geography) matters, and/or EU **CS-BCR / CRCF** is the commercial path. Prefer Verra/Isometric/Puro when CCP method labels, public CORC indexes, or non-EBC MRV cultures dominate. Full chooser: [[Puro vs Other Standards]] · [[Verra vs Isometric vs Puro]].
