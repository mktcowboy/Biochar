## 2. CORCX and CORCCHAR — snapshot (as of retrieval)

Source: [Puro Carbon Removal Indexes](https://puro.earth/buy-carbon-credits/removal-certificate-corc/carbon-removal-indexes/), retrieved **2026-09-14**:

| Index | Scope | Aug 2026 level | 1-month change | 6-month change | YTD change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CORCX** | Composite of **all** CORC pathway transactions | **€150.40** | +€12.05 (**+8.71%**) | +€10.29 (**+7.35%**) | +€15.31 (**+11.33%**) |
| **CORCCHAR** | **Biochar** CORC transactions only | **€135.35** | +€6.12 (**+4.74%**) | +€3.81 (**+2.74%**) | +€2.50 (**+1.88%**) |

### How to read them

1. **Transaction-based:** Indexes track **reported near-term CORC transactions** under a Nasdaq methodology—not a theoretical model price.
2. **Puro-only:** Levels apply to **CORCs** under the Puro Standard. They are **not** quotes for Verra [[Verra VM0044]] VCUs, [[Isometric Biochar Protocol]] credits, or [[CSI Global Biochar C-Sink]] C-sinks.
3. **Near-term bias:** Designed for price discovery on inventory that trades soon—not a perfect proxy for 10-year offtake strike prices.
4. **CORCCHAR vs CORCX:** In Aug 2026, biochar sat **~€15 below** the all-CORC composite. That gap can reflect pathway mix (other CORC methods in CORCX), liquidity, and timing—**not** a global “biochar discount” law.
5. **EU benchmark caveats:** Puro notes potential qualification as a benchmark under Regulation (EU) 2016/1011 for certain financial uses—read the factsheet before referencing in contracts.
6. **More indexes planned** as other methodologies gain transaction density (Puro page statement).

Full commercial context (fees, offtake waiver, liquidity days): [[Puro Pricing and CORC Indexes]] · [[Puro.earth Overview]].

## 4. Index vs bilateral offtake pricing

| Dimension | CORCCHAR / spot CORCs | Multi-year offtakes |
|---|---|---|
| Transparency | Public €/t index level | Unit price usually **confidential** ([[Historical Deals]]) |
| Timing | Near-term reported trades | Deliveries over 5–10+ years |
| Risk premium | Reflects current inventory | Embeds construction, feedstock, delivery, FX, offtake optionality |
| Quality add-ons | Standard CORC rules | May add dMRV (Carbonfuture), Forest Monitoring, CCP labels, 1000y modules |
| Fee overlays | Buyer pays retirement **€0.25/CORC** unless waived | Waivers possible for multi-year offtakes reported in MyPuro ([[Puro Pricing and CORC Indexes]]) |

**Practical rule:** Use CORCCHAR for **directional** Puro biochar price discovery; use press releases + CDR.fyi for **volumes**; assume **unknown** strike prices on megadeals unless a party discloses them.

[[Durable CDR Quality Tiers]] hypothesizes CCP / 1000-year / delivery premia. As of research date:

- **No** public table of BCR $/t by registry or CCP label in CDR.fyi abridged posts.
