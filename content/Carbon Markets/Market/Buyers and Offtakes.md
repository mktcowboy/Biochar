# Buyers and Offtakes

## 1. Demand structure at a glance

| Feature | Public signal | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Unique BCR purchasers | **290** (since 2022) | [Snapshot 2025](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/biochar-carbon-removal-market-snapshot-2025) |
| Top-4 BCR share | Microsoft + Google + BCG + JPMorgan ≈ **57%** | [Snapshot 2025](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/biochar-carbon-removal-market-snapshot-2025) |
| Microsoft BCR share | ≈ **46%** of cumulative BCR contracted | [Snapshot 2025](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/biochar-carbon-removal-market-snapshot-2025) |
| Long tail | Majority of purchasers **<100 t**; median ~**73–90 t** | [Snapshot 2025](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/biochar-carbon-removal-market-snapshot-2025) |
| Entry method | BCR is the **most common** first durable CDR purchase by buyer count (~5× next method) | [Snapshot 2025](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/biochar-carbon-removal-market-snapshot-2025) |
| Q1 2026 Microsoft share | **~43%** of durable CDR contracted that quarter | [Q1 2026](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2026-q1-durable-cdr-market-update-from-promise-to-proof) |
| Intermediaries | **74%** of Q1 2026 tonnes facilitated by intermediaries | [Q1 2026](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2026-q1-durable-cdr-market-update-from-promise-to-proof) |

**Takeaway:** A few catalytic buyers set megatonne headlines; a long tail of small trials creates the unique-buyer count. Strategy shifts by Microsoft (or a small peer set) can move the market—see [[Risks and Controversies]].

| Buyer | BCR share of CDR (Snapshot) | Stated rationale themes |
|---|---|---|
| **Swiss Re** | **99%** BCR | Integrity, durability, scalability, social co-benefits; geographic diversification |

## 3. Why these buyers choose BCR

Synthesized from public statements collected in Snapshot 2025 (not invented):

1. **Ready-now** — shorter path from contract to delivery than many engineered peers ([[Market Volumes and Pricing]]).
2. **Cost-efficient relative to DAC/BECCS** — qualitative consensus; exact premiums unpublished.
3. **Start-small optionality** — trials under 100 t lower organizational barriers.
4. **MRV / standard trust** — Puro CORCs, Isometric modules, CCP labels, dMRV (Carbonfuture).
5. **Co-benefits** — soil, air quality, community, waste diversion (deal-specific).
6. **Permanence framing** — ≥100y / several centuries / 1000y depending on pathway ([[Durable CDR Quality Tiers]] · [[Permanence Science for Credits]]).

Nasdaq additionally frames purchases as supporting marketplace scale and UN SDG co-benefits (Snapshot).

## 4. Notable offtake clusters (narrative)

### Microsoft–Exomad–Carbonfuture (Bolivia)

- **Dec 2023:** >**32,000 t** via Carbonfuture MRV+; Puro Standard; delivery by Jun 2024 ([Carbonfuture](https://www.carbonfuture.com/project-showcase/carbonfuture-announces-innovative-offtake-collaboration-with-exomad-green-and-microsoft-for-large-scale-biochar-carbon-removal)).
- **May 2025:** ≥**1.24 Mt** / 10 years; Puro Biochar Methodology; Forest Monitoring Center + dMRV contractually embedded ([Exomad](https://www.exomadgreen.com/post/exomad-green-announces-world-s-largest-biochar-carbon-removal-agreement-with-microsoft-tracked-by-c)).

### Microsoft–Liferaft–Supercritical (US Midwest)

- **Mar 2026:** **1 Mt** CRUs / 10 years; Iowa & Illinois; compost-blended biochar; ICROA-endorsed registry (unnamed in release) ([Liferaft](https://www.liferaft.cc/news/liferaft-microsoft-announcement)).

### Google biochar book

- **Jan 2025:** **100 kt** each from Varaha and Charm biochar by 2030 ([Google](https://blog.google/feed/were-announcing-our-first-partnerships-to-scale-biochar-for-co2-removal/)).
- **Mar 2026:** **200 ktCO₂e** from AMP by 2030; MSW organics → biochar ([AMP](https://ampsortation.com/articles/google-biochar-carbon-credits)).

### Altitude–Empacar (Bolivia CORCs)

- **Mar 2026:** **>305 kt** CORCs; Puro or equivalent ([Altitude](https://www.altitudecarbon.com/news/altitude-partners-with-empacar)).

### Near-term inventory: Supercritical–Exomad

- **Apr 2026:** **Up to 500 kt** over 3 years; remaining 2026 inventory + 2027–28; Puro ([Supercritical](https://gosupercritical.com/resources/press/exomad-green-supercritical-agreement)).

### High-durability niche: Microsoft–Carba–Isometric

- **Apr 2025:** **44 kt** / 5 years; landfill burial; Isometric 1,000-year durability ([Carba](https://carba.com/press/carba-announces-5-year-carbon-removal-credit-purchase-agreement-with-microsoft)).

## 5. Concentration risk & market fragility

| Risk | Evidence | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer concentration | Microsoft ~46% cumulative BCR ([Snapshot](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/biochar-carbon-removal-market-snapshot-2025)); ~43% of Q1 2026 durable CDR ([Q1 2026](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2026-q1-durable-cdr-market-update-from-promise-to-proof)) | Demand shock if strategy/pacing changes |
| Slow unique-buyer growth | +7% unique purchasers in 2024; first-time buyers −18% ([YIR](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2024-year-in-review)) | Volume growth ≠ broad market deepening |
| Delivery lag | ~22% of cumulative contracted BCR delivered by end-Q2 2025 ([Snapshot](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/biochar-carbon-removal-market-snapshot-2025)) | Forward books can outrun physical capacity |
| Intermediary dependence | 74% of Q1 2026 tonnes ([Q1 2026](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2026-q1-durable-cdr-market-update-from-promise-to-proof)) | Diligence quality and fee stacks matter for mid-market buyers |

Broader risk map: [[Risks and Controversies]]. Volume math: [[Market Volumes and Pricing]].
