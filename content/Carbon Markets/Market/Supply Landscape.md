# Supply Landscape

Public-facing map of **biochar CDR suppliers and projects** that appear in CDR.fyi reports and company press. Research date **2026-09-14**. **No private financials**, unpublished capacities, or invented delivery curves.

## 1. Market structure (supply side)

| Pattern | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Extreme supplier concentration | **Exomad Green ~60%** of BCR tonnes *sold* since 2022 | [Snapshot 2025](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/biochar-carbon-removal-market-snapshot-2025) |
| Global South delivery leadership | Top 4 suppliers by tonnes sold were Global South–based; **74%** of tonnes sold | Snapshot 2025 |
| Delivery leaders ≈ sales leaders | Top 4 by tonnes sold also among top by tonnes delivered | Snapshot 2025 |
| Small-order delivery paradox | Suppliers with many small orders have delivered more than sparse large-order peers (to date) | Snapshot 2025 |
| New megatonne entrants 2026 | Liferaft, Empacar, AMP first appear at 100 kt–1 Mt scale | [Q1 2026](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2026-q1-durable-cdr-market-update-from-promise-to-proof) + press |
| Near-term inventory scarcity narrative | Operational projects’ near-term tonnes framed as constrained vs long-dated promises | [Supercritical–Exomad Apr 2026](https://gosupercritical.com/resources/press/exomad-green-supercritical-agreement) |

Only **36%** of durable CDR suppliers listed on CDR.fyi had a registered sale as of the 2024 YIR framing—broad supplier lists ≠ bankable supply ([YIR](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2024-year-in-review)).

## 2. Major suppliers appearing in public data

### Exomad Green (Bolivia)

| Field | Public fact |
|---|---|
| Role | Largest BCR supplier by tonnes sold (~**60%** since 2022); leading durable CDR *deliverer* in multiple CDR.fyi periods |
| Cumulative sold | **>2.35 Mt** after Q1 2026 Supercritical + Senken deals ([CDR.fyi Q1 2026](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2026-q1-durable-cdr-market-update-from-promise-to-proof)) |
| Produced to date | **>320,000 t** durable CDR (Apr 2026 Supercritical/Exomad release) |
| Facilities | Concepción & Riberalta (expanded); third under construction; two more planned (May 2025 release) |
| Pathway | Forestry residues (else open-burned) → biochar → local agricultural soils |
| Certification | **Puro.earth** Biochar Methodology; Carbonfuture MRV+ on Microsoft deals |
| Flagship offtakes | Microsoft **>32 kt** (2023); Microsoft **≥1.24 Mt** / 10y (2025); Supercritical **up to 500 kt** / 3y (2026); Senken **105 kt** (Q1 2026) |
| Ambition cited | **1 Mt CO₂ / yr by 2027** |
| Sources | [Exomad May 2025](https://www.exomadgreen.com/post/exomad-green-announces-world-s-largest-biochar-carbon-removal-agreement-with-microsoft-tracked-by-c); [Supercritical Apr 2026](https://gosupercritical.com/resources/press/exomad-green-supercritical-agreement); Snapshot / Q1 2026 |

### Liferaft (United States)

| Field | Public fact |
|---|---|
| Role | First-time supplier with **1 Mt** Microsoft offtake (Q1 2026) |
| Geography | Iowa & Illinois (West Liberty / Muscatine County called out) |
| Pathway | Ag + municipal biomass → pyrolysis → biochar–compost mix → approved soil uses |
| Commercial | Facilitated by Supercritical; ICROA-endorsed registry (unnamed in release) |
| Source | [Liferaft Mar 2026](https://www.liferaft.cc/news/liferaft-microsoft-announcement) |

### Empacar S.A. (Bolivia)

| Field | Public fact |
|---|---|
| Role | Industrial circular-economy company entering BCR; **>305 kt** CORC procurement by Altitude |
| Pathway | Forestry & agricultural residues; industrial pyrolysis; Bioflux program support |
| Registry intent | **Puro.earth or equivalent** |
| Scale signal | Multiple sites under development; secondary coverage cites ~**70 kt/yr** design for Ascención de Guarayos and first issuance **2027**—treat secondary figures cautiously |
| Source | [Altitude Mar 2026](https://www.altitudecarbon.com/news/altitude-partners-with-empacar) |

### AMP / Commonwealth Sortation (United States)

| Field | Public fact |
|---|---|
| Role | AI MSW sortation → organics → biochar; Google **200 ktCO₂e** by 2030 |
| Geography | Southeastern Public Service Authority (SPSA), Virginia — 20-year contract; up to **540,000 t** MSW / yr processed |
| End use (initial) | Landfill daily cover; future construction/cement pathways eyed |
| Co-work | Methane-impact quantification frameworks with Google |
| Source | [AMP Mar 2026](https://ampsortation.com/articles/google-biochar-carbon-credits) |

### Varaha (India)

| Field | Public fact |
|---|---|
| Role | Google **100 kt** by 2030; cumulative sold **>500 kt** ([CDR.fyi Q1 2026](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2026-q1-durable-cdr-market-update-from-promise-to-proof)); **$20M** funding Q1 2026 |
| Pathway | Invasive plants / agricultural waste → biochar; smallholder networks (Reuters/Google coverage) |
| Sources | [Google Jan 2025](https://blog.google/feed/were-announcing-our-first-partnerships-to-scale-biochar-for-co2-removal/); [Q1 2026](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2026-q1-durable-cdr-market-update-from-promise-to-proof) |

### Charm Industrial (United States) — biochar path

| Field | Public fact |
|---|---|
| Role | Google **100 kt** *biochar* purchase by 2030 (distinct from Charm’s bio-oil sequestration business) |
| Note | Also sells other BiCRS products; do not conflate all Charm volume with BCR |
| Source | [Google Jan 2025](https://blog.google/feed/were-announcing-our-first-partnerships-to-scale-biochar-for-co2-removal/) |

### Carba (United States)

| Field | Public fact |
|---|---|
| Role | Microsoft **44 kt** / 5 years; landfill burial / daily cover |
| Registry | **Isometric**; 1,000-year durability; low-oxygen burial module |
| Other | DOE Carbon Negative Shot Pilot **$7M** grant referenced |
| Source | [Carba Apr 2025](https://carba.com/press/carba-announces-5-year-carbon-removal-credit-purchase-agreement-with-microsoft) |

## 6. Gaps / honesty box

2. Private offtake stacks beyond press releases.
3. Exact Puro vs Verra vs Isometric vs CSI **share of delivered tonnes** not in abridged CDR.fyi posts.
4. Financial health, capex, and margins—out of scope unless disclosed.
5. Artisanal vs industrial segmentation deliberately collapsed in Snapshot 2025 (“biochar in its entirety”).
