# Market Volumes and Pricing

Chapter-length synthesis of **biochar carbon removal (BCR)** market volumes and price signals as of **2026-09-14**. Figures are from primary / high-signal public sources only—chiefly CDR.fyi abridged reports and Puro × Nasdaq indexes. **Do not treat this note as live market data**; re-pull sources before diligence.

## 1. How to read the three volume metrics

| Metric | What it measures | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Contracted / purchased** | Forward offtakes + spot sales reported to trackers | Demand & bankability; dominated by multi-year megadeals |
| **Delivered** | Tonnes actually issued / delivered ex-post | Operational proof; BCR leads all durable CDR methods here |
| **Retired** | Tonnes cancelled on a registry for a climate claim | Final use of the credit; liquidity + claim integrity |

CDR.fyi tracks durable removals (hundreds to thousands of years permanence). Coverage claim in Q1 2026 notes: **~97%+** of *publicly disclosed* durable CDR contract volume ([methodology caveats](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2026-q1-durable-cdr-market-update-from-promise-to-proof)).

## 2. Cumulative BCR volumes (Q1 2022 → H1 2025)

**Primary source:** [Biochar Carbon Removal Market Snapshot 2025](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/biochar-carbon-removal-market-snapshot-2025) (published **2025-09-09**).

### Headline table

| Metric | Figure | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cumulative BCR **contracted** | **3.04 Mt** | Q1 2022 through end-H1 2025 |
| Of which H1 2025 | **1.6 Mt** | Exceeded all prior periods combined since 2022 |
| Cumulative **delivered** | **683 kt** | End-Q2 2025 (*highlights also list 658 kt—prefer body 683 kt*) |
| Cumulative **retired** | **330 kt** | End-Q2 2025 (*highlights also list 302 kt—prefer body 330 kt*) |
| Delivered : purchased | ~**22%** (1 : 4.5) | Large forward books vs physical delivery |
| Retired : delivered | ~**50%** (1 : 2) | Half of delivered tonnes retired by end-Q2 2025 |
| Unique BCR purchasers | **290** | ~5× more buyers than next CDR method by buyer count |
| Avg volume / purchaser / year | **542 t (2022) → 762 t (H1 2025)** | Rising average order size |
| Ex-Microsoft avg deal size | **197 t → 278 t** | Still small vs megadeals |
| Median contracted | **90 t (2024) → 73 t (2025 YTD in snapshot)** | Majority of purchasers still <100 t |

### Growth narrative

- 2022–2024: purchase tonnage roughly **tripled** year-over-year; 2024 purchases exceeded all prior years combined.
- Q2 2025: largest BCR quarter ever, driven by Microsoft–Exomad Green **≥1.24 Mt** ([Exomad May 2025](https://www.exomadgreen.com/post/exomad-green-announces-world-s-largest-biochar-carbon-removal-agreement-with-microsoft-tracked-by-c); [[Historical Deals]]).
- Deliveries and retirements roughly **doubled** annually 2022–2024; Q1–Q2 2025 retirements were closer to flat vs year-ago quarters (+12% / +0.4%).

### BCR’s role inside durable CDR

| Share type | BCR share | Peer context |
|---|---|---|
| Durable CDR **deliveries** (since Q1 2022) | **~86%** | Delivery leader |
| Durable CDR **retirements** | **~92%** | Retirement leader |
| Durable CDR **contracted** | **~10%** | BECCS ~**75%** of contracted; DAC ~**9%** |

Interpretation: biochar is the **workhorse of delivered/retired tonnes** and the **default entry method for new buyers**, while BECCS dominates *headline contracted* megatonnes that may take years to deliver.

## 3. 2024 durable CDR context (pricing + delivery)

**Primary source:** [2024 Year in Review](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2024-year-in-review) (published **2025-02-14**; appendix note dated Feb 20, 2025).

| Metric | 2024 figure |
|---|---|
| Total durable CDR purchased | Almost **8 Mt** (+**78%** YoY) |
| Deliveries | **318.6 kt** (+**120%** YoY) |
| Delivery-to-booking ratio | **4.4%** |
| Biochar share of delivery volume | **86%** (and **83%** of 2020–2024 cumulative deliveries) |
| Weighted avg price (disclosed orders, **all methods**) | **$490 → $320 / tCO₂** (2023→2024) |
| Biochar price change | **+18%** YoY (exception vs most methods; **exact $/t not in abridged post**) |
| Mineralization price change | **+123%** YoY (peer comparison) |
| Purchaser concentration | Microsoft + Google + Frontier buyers **>75%**; Microsoft alone **~64%** (**5.1 Mt**) |
| Unique purchasers growth | Only **+7%** (202→216); first-time buyers **−18%** |

**Supplier geography for BCR deliveries (YIR):** Global South prominent—Exomad Green & Aperam Bioenergia (South America), Carboneers & Planboo (Africa), Varaha (India); US names include Wakefield Biochar and Pacific Biochar.

## 5. Price trends — index vs bilateral

### 5.1 Public index (Puro × Nasdaq)

Retrieved **2026-09-14** from [Puro Carbon Removal Indexes](https://puro.earth/buy-carbon-credits/removal-certificate-corc/carbon-removal-indexes/):

| Index | Aug 2026 level | 1-mo Δ | 6-mo Δ | YTD Δ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CORCX** (all CORC pathways) | **€150.40** | +€12.05 (**+8.71%**) | +€10.29 (**+7.35%**) | +€15.31 (**+11.33%**) |
| **CORCCHAR** (biochar CORCs) | **€135.35** | +€6.12 (**+4.74%**) | +€3.81 (**+2.74%**) | +€2.50 (**+1.88%**) |

Deep how-to-read: [[Price Indexes and Benchmarks]] · [[Puro Pricing and CORC Indexes]].

**CORCCHAR < CORCX** in this snapshot: biochar trades at a discount to the all-CORC composite on reported near-term Puro transactions—**not** proof of a global BCR discount vs Verra/Isometric/CSI.

### 5.2 Survey / disclosed-order pricing (CDR.fyi)

| Observation | Detail | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| All-method WAP ↓ | $490 → $320 / t (2023→2024) ([YIR](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2024-year-in-review)) | Mix of DAC, BECCS, EW, BCR, etc. |
| Biochar ↑ | **+18%** YoY ([YIR](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2024-year-in-review)) | Exact BCR mean **undisclosed** in abridged YIR |
| Narrow BCR band | YIR says biochar settled into a “relatively narrow price range” | No numeric band published in abridged post |
| Megadeal unit prices | Generally **not** in press releases | Major transparency gap |

### 5.3 Index vs bilateral offtakes

| Channel | Characteristics |
|---|---|
| **Index (CORCCHAR)** | Near-term reported CORC trades; transparent €/t level; Puro-only |
| **Spot platform inventory** | Certified tonnes; may track near index |
| **Multi-year offtakes** | Firm + optional volumes; prices usually confidential; may embed delivery risk, co-benefits, dMRV requirements |
| **Intermediated portfolios** | Supercritical, Senken, Watershed, Patch, Carbonfuture, etc.; 74% of Q1 2026 tonnes |

Science-side abatement cost ranges (e.g., literature ~$70–360/t) are **not** credit market prices—see [[Quantitative Benchmarks]].

### 5.4 Quality-tier effects on price

Public abridged sources **do not quantify** CCP-label or 1000-year-path premiums in $/t. Practical segmentation hypotheses (unverified price deltas):

1. Buyers requiring **CCP methodology labels today** may prefer [[Verra VM0044]] VCUs.
2. Buyers requiring **1000-year Ro / burial** may prefer [[Isometric Biochar Protocol]] (e.g., Carba–Microsoft).
3. Buyers optimizing **near-term delivery + CORCCHAR discovery** may prefer [[Puro CORC200+]] inventory.
4. Uncertified marketing claims should clear at **zero credit value** for neutralization accounting ([[Durable CDR Quality Tiers]]).

## 7. Why buyers choose BCR (volume implications)

From Snapshot business-case synthesis (Swiss Re, SEB, Tide, Nasdaq, and diversified majors):

1. **Rapid contracting → delivery** relative to DAC/BECCS.
2. **Lower prices** relative to many engineered methods (qualitative; exact differentials sparse).
3. Ability to **start small** (<100 t trials) — explains 290 unique purchasers.

These structural advantages explain why BCR can be ~10% of *contracted* durable CDR yet ~86–92% of *delivered/retired* tonnes.
