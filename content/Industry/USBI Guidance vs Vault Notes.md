# USBI Guidance vs Vault Notes

Explicit comparison between **U.S. Biochar Initiative / American Biochar Institute** practitioner guidance and this vault’s Science / Applications / Carbon Markets / Policy notes. Org context: [[US Biochar Initiative]]. Report date: **2026-09-16**.

**Method:** Only claims that appear in cited USBI/ABI URLs or PDFs are attributed to USBI/ABI. Vault claims keep their existing outbound URLs / DOIs. Where USBI is thinner or stronger, that is about **coverage depth**, not a judgment that one side is “wrong” without a sourced conflict.

---

## Snapshot TLDR

| Verdict | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Overall agreement** | Strong on charging/inoculation, site-specific rates, compost co-addition bands, NRCS CPS 336 as the U.S. farm incentive pathway, and IBI-aligned lab specs for soil use. |
| **What’s new / stronger at USBI–ABI** | Packaged practitioner PDFs (2026 suite), **ANSI/ASABE/USBI S668** testing standard, ABI+AFT NRCS funding FAQ packaging, **2025 U.S. Biochar Market Report** survey benchmarks, stormwater/poultry/concrete library breadth. |
| **What’s stronger in the vault** | Peer-reviewed mechanisms (immobilization, coatings, nitrate capture), BBF chemistry/biology chapters, registry MRV (Puro/Verra/Isometric/CSI), CDR.fyi deal/volume tracking, permanence science debates. |
| **Conflicts?** | No hard numerical contradiction found on the core farm practices. Unit-basis differences matter (USBI **tons/acre** & **% volume** vs NRCS **yd³/acre** scenarios; compost **% volume** vs some literature **% w/w**). Market figures from ABI survey ≠ CDR.fyi credit deliveries. |

## 4. Apparent tensions / unit traps (not true contradictions)

1. **Tons vs cubic yards:** USBI leads with **t/ac**; NRCS payment scenarios lead with **yd³/ac**. Convert only with measured bulk density/moisture ([NRCS FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).
2. **Compost % volume vs % weight:** USBI compost sheet is **volume**-based ([Compost PDF](https://biochar.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Biochar-Guidelines-Compost-2026-Draft-V1.pdf)); some vault literature bands are **w/w** (Pacific / Antonangelo). Same intent, different basis — don’t mix without density.
3. **“1–10 t/ac consistent positives” vs temperate meta-analyses:** Vault yield metas show **context dependence** (weaker mean temperate responses — [[Tropical vs Temperate Yields|compares tropical vs temperate yields]] https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/aa67bd). USBI’s own poorer-soil framing and the vault’s inoculation chapter reconcile this: raw high-C wood char on fertile temperate soils is exactly where charging matters most.
4. **Market numbers:** ABI **151 kt physical biochar (2025 survey)** ≠ CDR.fyi **credit tonnes delivered**. Keep [[US Biochar Market Report 2025|surveys the US biochar market]] and [[Market Volumes and Pricing]] side-by-side, not merged.
5. **IBI seal vs CPS 336:** ABI funding guide emphasizes IBI seal/criteria; NRCS FAQ stresses **spec sheet still required** even with certification — vault already captured ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).
