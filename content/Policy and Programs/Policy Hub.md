# Policy Hub

Public programs, practice standards, and product certification that sit beside voluntary carbon markets. Distinct from registry methodologies ([[Carbon Markets Hub]]): policy here means government ag programs, inventory rules, and quality labels that shape how biochar is produced and used.

## Domain purpose

- **Practice / incentive programs** (e.g. US NRCS) can pay for soil application without issuing tradable CDR credits.
- **Product standards** (EBC, WBC, IBI) define material quality and safety thresholds used by markets and buyers.
- **Inventory / climate policy** (IPCC appendix; regional frameworks mentioned in market notes) affects national accounting—not the same as project-level credits.

Keep these layers separate when reading methodologies or offtake contracts.
