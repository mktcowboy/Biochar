# Product Standards (EBC / IBI / WBC)

Hub on **product quality standards** for biochar — material definition, contaminant limits, and use-class labeling. These are **not** carbon-credit methodologies; they sit underneath (or alongside) registry methods.

**CSI link:** [[CSI Global Biochar C-Sink]] **requires** EBC or WBC certification before a Global Biochar C-Sink can be issued ([v3.3 §1.3](https://www.carbon-standards.com/docs/transfer/4000039EN.pdf)). Puro / Verra / Isometric generally expect laboratory characterization without mandating the EBC/WBC *label*.

| **Practice standards** | [[NRCS CPS 336 and US Ag]] | Farm conservation practice, not tradable credits |

## EBC / WBC (Carbon Standards stack)

| Item | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Operator | Same CSI ecosystem as Global Biochar C-Sink | [carbon-standards.com](https://www.carbon-standards.com/) |
| **EBC** | European Biochar Certificate — EU-focused product cert | [service-492](https://www.carbon-standards.com/en/standards/service-492~production-of-biochar.html) · [european-biochar.org](https://www.european-biochar.org/) |
| **EBC current PDF** | **Standard 10.5E** (doc stamp 15.09.2025) | [4000093EN.pdf](https://www.carbon-standards.com/docs/transfer/4000093EN.pdf) |
| Biomass positive list | Permissible feedstocks | [4000095EN.pdf](https://www.carbon-standards.com/docs/transfer/4000095EN.pdf) |
| **WBC** | World Biochar Certificate — parallel track outside/alongside EBC geography | [service-514](https://www.carbon-standards.com/en/standards/service-514~production-of-biochar.html) |
| Process | Technical pre-audit → batch lab analysis (endorsed labs + DCC ring trial) → annual on-site inspection; batches ≤1 year | service-492 |
| System providers | Endorsed equipment vendors shorten pre-audit; default CH₄ factors | [fact sheet](https://www.carbon-standards.com/docs/transfer/4000099EN.pdf) |

Use classes (agriculture, materials, feed-related, etc.) and contaminant tables live in the EBC/WBC guideline PDFs — cite those before quoting numeric limits in other notes. Vault already carries practice thresholds in [[Properties]] and [[Contaminants and Quality]].

### How CSI C-Sink consumes EBC/WBC

1. EBC/WBC certificate + QR / Global Biochar Tool batch record  
2. Analytical values for C-sink size (C_org, DM, H/C_org, …)  
3. Optional **Ro** / **HyPy** on the **same batch sample** for [[CSI GPC vs SPC Permanence Classes|upper persistence class]]  
4. Processor/trader roles also EBC/WBC-certified for chain-of-custody  

Deep: [[CSI Overview]] · [[CSI MRV and Emission Portfolio]] · [[CSI Global Biochar C-Sink]]

Guidelines index on the legacy EBC site points to CSI-hosted docs: [EBC/WBC guidelines](https://www.european-biochar.org/en/ct/2-EBC-and-WBC-guidelines-documents).

## IBI Biochar Standards

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Publisher | International Biochar Initiative |
| Role | Widely referenced product / characterization framework (esp. North America) |
| URL | https://biochar-international.org/ |
| Lab practice | ABI *Interpreting Lab Reports* points to IBI v2.1 — [PDF](https://biochar.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Biochar-Guidelines-Lab-Report-2026.pdf) · [[US Biochar Initiative]] |

IBI is **not** a CSI prerequisite. US producers often combine IBI-style lab packages with ANSI/ASABE/USBI S668 methods ([[ANSI ASABE USBI S668]]) even when targeting Puro/Verra/Isometric rather than EBC.
