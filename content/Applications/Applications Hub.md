# Applications Hub

Where biochar goes after production: **soil and agriculture** (primary chapter set), greenhouse-gas flux effects, and materials / built-environment uses. Application pathway shapes agronomic outcomes and how carbon credits treat storage (soil vs concrete/asphalt/composites).

Report context: **2026-09-16** (USBI/ABI comparison added).

## Domain purpose

Science and markets both care *where* char is placed:

- **Soil** — yield effects, water/CEC/liming, N₂O/CH₄ fluxes, priming, long-term persistence ([[Permanence]])
- **Built / materials** — concrete, asphalt, polymers; eligibility and MRV differ ([[Materials and Built Uses]])
- **Policy** — US farm practice standards (NRCS CPS 336) sit alongside voluntary carbon markets ([[NRCS CPS 336 and US Ag]])
