# On-farm Trial Design

How to structure **credible on-farm biochar trials** so results can inform rate, blend, and economics decisions—without inventing statistics. Pair with [[Field Rates by System]], [[Crop Yield Effects]], [[Agronomic Economics]], and named precedents in [[Real World Soil Projects]].

Report context: **2026-09-16**.

---

## 1. Why design matters

Meta-analyses show **large context dependence** (soil pH, texture, climate, fertilizer co-application) ([[Crop Productivity Meta-analysis|meta-analyzes crop productivity]] · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agee.2011.08.015); [[Tropical vs Temperate Yields|compares tropical vs temperate yields]] · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/aa67bd); [[Global GHG and Yield Patterns|global GHG and yield patterns]] · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/ldr.4868)). A single unreplicated strip cannot overturn those patterns—but a well-logged strip trial *can* answer “does **this** char × **this** blend × **this** rate work **here**?”

Jeffery et al. (2016) CH₄ meta required randomised, replicated designs for inclusion (**42** of 62 screened studies; **189** pairwise comparisons) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.soilbio.2016.07.021))—a reminder that weak designs get filtered from science, and weak farm demos get filtered from lenders.

## 2. Minimum viable farm trial

| Element | Sourced rationale / example | URL |
|---|---|---|
| **Control** | Same fertility, irrigation, timing minus biochar | Oasis four-way: control / compost / biochar / combo https://pacificbiochar.com/wp-content/uploads/Oasis-Vineyard-Trial_-5th-harvest_-Final-Draft_12.12.2023.pdf |
| **Replication** | Gady Farm USBI case: **4 replicates × 2 seasons** | https://farmlandinfo.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2023/06/usbi-biochar-guidelines-for-ag-application.pdf |
| **Randomisation / blocking** | Steiner poultry composting: complete-block, 3 repeats of 3 treatments | https://doi.org/10.2134/jeq2009.0337 |
| **Rate chosen a priori** | Tie to NRCS scenario or literature analogue | [[Field Rates by System]] · FAQ https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf |
| **Charge state documented** | Raw vs inoculated vs co-composted is a **treatment**, not a footnote | [[Biochar Inoculation for Fertilizer]] · Kammann contrast https://doi.org/10.1038/srep11080 |
| **Multi-year** | Oasis needed **5 harvests** for economics narrative; Woody persistence claims cite Woolf | Oasis PDF · https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.1c02425 |
| **Same inputs otherwise** | Oasis held irrigation/fertilizer constant (so did **not** monetize water/fertilizer savings) | Oasis PDF · [[Oasis Vineyard Biochar Trial|vineyard biochar field trial]] |

Earth Foundries facility demo used **one pile per treatment, one cycle**—treat as demonstration, not inferential stats ([[Napa Co-composting CASP Report|reports Napa CASP co-composting]]).

## 3. Treatment ideas that answer real questions

1. **Raw vs charged vs co-composted** at equal biochar mass (Kammann logic — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep11080)).
2. **Broadcast vs banded** at equal total t/ac (placement efficiency — [[Incorporation Topdress Banding]]).
3. **Biochar ± fertilizer** factorial (Ye et al. 2020 synergy framing — [PDF](https://www.css.cornell.edu/faculty/lehmann/publ/SoilUseManage%2036%2C%202-18%202020%20Ye.pdf)).
4. **NRCS scenario rates** vs half-rate enriched BBF (Melo mean **0.9 t ha⁻¹** — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11104-021-05276-2)).
5. **Compost alone / biochar alone / combo** (Oasis design).

## 5. Statistics without overclaiming

- Prefer **confidence intervals / paired contrasts** over single-year % without error bars.
- Report **absolute** yields alongside % vs control (Oasis cumulative t/ac table is a model — [PDF](https://pacificbiochar.com/wp-content/uploads/Oasis-Vineyard-Trial_-5th-harvest_-Final-Draft_12.12.2023.pdf)).
- For economics, stress-test **crop price** and **zero yield response** (Galinato caution for temperate wheat — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enpol.2011.07.035); [[Agronomic Economics]]).
- Do not average unreplicated strips into a “meta.”
