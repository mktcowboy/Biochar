# Incorporation Topdress Banding

**Where** biochar goes in the soil profile and row: broadcast + tillage, banding / seed-row, deep incorporation, no-till / drill / injection, pasture topdress, and composting co-batching. Rates: [[Field Rates by System]]. Gear: [[Equipment and Timing]]. Hub: [[Application Rates and Methods]].

Report context: **2026-09-16**.

---

## 1. Why placement matters

NRCS FAQ: incorporate “the same ways soil amendments are normally incorporated” including tillage, trenching, no-till drill, permanent beds, surface apply, or mixed into water; **best results** with incorporation; surface-only applications risk wind/water loss, especially on slopes ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf) · [[Soil Carbon Amendment FAQ|answers CPS 336 planning questions]]). USBI: incorporate when possible to limit wind erosion ([Ag Guidelines](https://farmlandinfo.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2023/06/usbi-biochar-guidelines-for-ag-application.pdf) · [[Farm Biochar Application Guidelines|guides farm biochar application]]).

Root-zone placement lets **low doses** of enriched chars work ([[Urine-Enhanced Biochar Field Trial|urine-enhanced biochar field trial]] · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/agriculture5030723)).

## 2. Broadcast + incorporation

Standard manure/lime spreaders followed by disc, rotary, or other tillage. USBI photo/case language includes Iowa corn broadcast then incorporate ([Guidelines](https://farmlandinfo.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2023/06/usbi-biochar-guidelines-for-ag-application.pdf)). Gady Farm WA: lime spreader then rototill to **4-inch** depth before winter wheat at **8 t/ac** biochar ([same PDF](https://farmlandinfo.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2023/06/usbi-biochar-guidelines-for-ag-application.pdf)).

NRCS notes deep tillage is a **one-time** disturbance relative to multi-century char C ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).

## 3. Banding / seed-row / planting-row

Concentrate char where roots explore.

| Example | Placement | URL |
|---|---|---|
| Oasis Vineyard | GPS-ripped delve down vine row; winged plow to **~30"** in bowl ~**2 ft wide × 2.5 ft deep** | https://pacificbiochar.com/wp-content/uploads/Oasis-Vineyard-Trial_-5th-harvest_-Final-Draft_12.12.2023.pdf · [[Oasis Vineyard Biochar Trial|vineyard biochar field trial]] |
| USBI perennial guidance | Incorporate directly into planting row | https://farmlandinfo.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2023/06/usbi-biochar-guidelines-for-ag-application.pdf |
| Exomad | Planting-row incorporation and trenching for perennials | https://www.exomadgreen.com/biochar |
| Nepal urine-biochar | Localized root-zone pits with compost | https://doi.org/10.3390/agriculture5030723 |

Banding cuts total tonnes vs whole-field broadcast at the same in-band concentration—central to [[Agronomic Economics]] and [[Fertilizer Displacement]].

## 4. Deep incorporation / redevelopment

Dominus Estate: excavator / earthworks cultivating biochar to **36"** after fallow redevelopment at **25 t/ac** ([case study](https://pacificbiochar.com/case-study-biochar-application-with-dominus-estate-in-napa-valley-after-2-years-fallow/)). Appropriate for vineyard/orchard redevelopment—not annual no-till.

## 5. No-till / drill / prills / injection / fertigation

- Crush/screen or compress to **prills** for drills; inject biochar–manure mixes; fertigate micronized suspensions ([NRCS FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).
- Plugging risk if particle size mismatches equipment (FAQ).
- Exomad lists liquid injection / fertigation for perennials ([Exomad](https://www.exomadgreen.com/biochar)).
- USBI: incorporate into liquid slurry before field application where manure systems allow ([Guidelines](https://farmlandinfo.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2023/06/usbi-biochar-guidelines-for-ag-application.pdf)).

## 6. Pasture topdress

CPS 336 may be planned on **pasture**; **not** on native rangeland except to facilitate CPS 550 or 342. Surface topdress without incorporation increases loss risk—follow slope/runoff restrictions ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)). Brazil pasture research + CBA: [Latawiec et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-47647-x). USBI notes grazing can help incorporation of surface applications in some forage systems ([Guidelines](https://farmlandinfo.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2023/06/usbi-biochar-guidelines-for-ag-application.pdf)).

## 7. Composting facility co-batching

Add biochar **into** composting (not only field blend). Earth Foundries: **10% by volume** ([PDF](https://www.earthfoundriesinc.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Earth-Foundries-Green-Compost-Biochar-Composting-Project-Report-2023.pdf)). SEC/Napa pathway: https://sonomabiocharinitiative.org/sec-and-napa-recycling-pyrolysis-startup/. See [[Biochar Compost and Manure Blends]], [[Farm Co-composting Practice]].
