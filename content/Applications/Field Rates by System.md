# Field Rates by System

**How much** biochar (and blends) to plan by system type—NRCS scenarios, research spans, enriched fertilizers, vineyards, pastures, and CDR-inventory thresholds. Placement: [[Incorporation Topdress Banding]]. Gear/calendar: [[Equipment and Timing]]. Trials: [[On-farm Trial Design]]. Hub: [[Application Rates and Methods]]. Economics: [[Agronomic Economics]].

Report context: **2026-09-16**. Every rate below is sourced; convert units with measured bulk density.

---

## 1. Principles before picking a number

1. Soil- and product-specific (soil tests + amendment lab sheets) — [NRCS FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf) · [[Soil Carbon Amendment FAQ|answers CPS 336 planning questions]].
2. Charge / inoculate when agronomic response is a goal ([CPS 336 2022](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2022-11/336-NHCP-CPS-Soil-Carbon-Amendment-2022.pdf); [USBI](https://farmlandinfo.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2023/06/usbi-biochar-guidelines-for-ag-application.pdf)).
3. Separate **CDR mass** goals from **agronomic optimum** ([[Agronomic Economics]], [[Matrix Pathways for Credits]]).
4. Unit caveat: dry weight of **1 yd³** biochar roughly **135–540 lb** ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).

## 2. US NRCS CPS 336 planning rates

| Context | Rate | Source |
|---|---|---|
| General planning start | **4–10 cubic yards per acre** | https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf |
| FY2024 100% biochar scenario | **4 yd³/ac** | same FAQ Table 1 |
| 60% biochar / 40% compost | **2.4 yd³ biochar + 2.2 t compost /ac** | same |
| 40% biochar / 60% compost | **1.6 yd³ + 3.3 t /ac** | same |
| 20% biochar / 80% compost | **0.8 yd³ + 4.4 t /ac** | same |
| 80% biochar / 20% compost | **3.2 yd³ + 1.1 t /ac** | same |
| Compost off-site only | **3 t compost /ac** | same |

USBI Ag Guidelines cite field application rates of **1–10 tons/acre** as a practical span and note NRCS **4 cu yd/ac** as a lowest-effective planning cite in that document ([Farmland Info PDF](https://farmlandinfo.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2023/06/usbi-biochar-guidelines-for-ag-application.pdf) · [[Farm Biochar Application Guidelines|guides farm biochar application]]; ABI 2026 ag PDF https://biochar.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Biochar-Guidelines-for-Agriculture-Applications-2026.pdf).

## 4. Matching rate to climate / soil expectation

Yield metas (full tables [[Crop Yield Effects]]):

- Stronger mean responses in **acidic**, **coarse**, **tropical/subtropical**, low-fertility soils ([[Crop Productivity Meta-analysis|meta-analyzes crop productivity]] · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agee.2011.08.015); [[Tropical vs Temperate Yields|compares tropical vs temperate yields]] tropical ~**+25%** vs temperate little mean benefit — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/aa67bd)).
- Temperate fertile systems often need **blends**, **banding**, or **policy/CDR stack** to pencil ([[Agronomic Economics]], Galinato et al. 2011 [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enpol.2011.07.035)).
- Biochar **+ fertilizer** field synergy ~**+15%** vs fertilizer alone ([[Fertilizer-Controlled Yield Meta-analysis|meta-analyzes yields with fertilizer controls]] · [PDF](https://www.css.cornell.edu/faculty/lehmann/publ/SoilUseManage%2036%2C%202-18%202020%20Ye.pdf)).

## 6. Converting yd³/ac ↔ t/ac (method, not a universal factor)

NRCS FAQ dry weight of **1 yd³** ≈ **135–540 lb** ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).

Example arithmetic (illustrative bounds, not a recommendation):

- At **135 lb/yd³** dry: 4 yd³/ac ≈ **540 lb/ac** ≈ **0.27 t/ac**
- At **540 lb/yd³** dry: 4 yd³/ac ≈ **2,160 lb/ac** ≈ **1.08 t/ac**

Wet as-applied masses (Oasis biochar 38% moisture) are higher than dry tonnes ([Oasis PDF](https://pacificbiochar.com/wp-content/uploads/Oasis-Vineyard-Trial_-5th-harvest_-Final-Draft_12.12.2023.pdf)). Always use the **batch** bulk density and moisture.

Woolf’s **10 Mg C ha⁻¹** N₂O threshold is carbon mass, not biochar mass—divide by FC (e.g. wood FC ~0.7–0.81 in Woolf Table 2) to estimate biochar tonnes ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.1c02425) · [[Biochar Soil GHG Inventory Model|models soil GHG from biochar]]).

## 7. Rate × inoculation matrix (planning aid)

| Goal | Typical rate class | Conditioning |
|---|---|---|
| CPS 336 cost-share compliance | FAQ scenarios 0.8–4 yd³ biochar/ac ± compost | Inoculate per standard |
| Temperate agronomy on fertile soil | Start low; prefer blend/band | Charge 1–3 weeks or COMBI |
| Acid/coarse/tropical yield response | Literature often multi-t ha⁻¹ | Still prefer charged if woody char |
| BBF / urine-char fertilizer | ≪2 t ha⁻¹ root-zone | Enrichment required |
| CDR mass maximization | May exceed agronomic optimum | Separate fertility program; watch EC |

Sources: FAQ · USBI · Schmidt · Melo · Jeffery — URLs in tables above.
