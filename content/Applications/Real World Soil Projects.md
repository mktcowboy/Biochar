## Carbon-credit projects with verified soil end-use

### Exomad Green — Concepción / Riberalta, Bolivia (Puro.earth)

- **Registry:** Puro facility page states CORC **100+** durability; biochar from sawmill hardwood residues pyrolyzed ~**600 °C**; biochar **donated to local Indigenous communities** for agricultural soil enhancement ([Puro — Exomad Green, Concepción](https://puro.earth/cdr-credit-suppliers/facilities/exomad-green-concepcion/)).
- **Issuance (facility table on Puro page):** 2023 **21,252**; 2024 **58,344**; 2025 **102,458**; 2026 **91,457** CORCs (as published on page at fetch time).
- **Ag guidance:** mix with inoculant; site-specific rates; planting-row / volume-% examples ([Exomad biochar](https://www.exomadgreen.com/biochar)).
- **Field study (company report with CIAT; press 2025-07-04):** Santa Cruz trials, 4 t ha⁻¹ once — corn **+15%** (up to **+32%** with fertilizer + microbes); beans **+13%** (up to **+25%**) ([PDF](https://www.exomadgreen.com/_files/ugd/8b0a69_153ad8a2b8244bfc8a136176be56a408.pdf); [Bioenergy International](https://bioenergyinternational.com/biochar-field-study-reveals-significant-crop-yield-improvements-in-bolivia/)). Yield ANOVA not significant and no fertilizer-only control — see [[Exomad Green Corn and Bean Field Study]].
- **MRV partner case study:** [Carbonfuture — Exomad](https://www.carbonfuture.earth/case-studies/exomad-green).

**Verification note:** Soil end-use is explicit on Puro and company pages (donation / farm application)—not a non-soil-only sink.

### Charm Range & Plains Biochar — USA (Isometric)

- **Registry project:** Charm co-produces bio-oil and biochar (Fort Lupton, CO) from wildfire-mitigation woody biomass; “We transport biochar via truck to various sites where it is **sequestered via land application**” ([Isometric project](https://registry.isometric.com/project/prj_1JN6XNWDQ1S0BSN7)).
- Crediting period **01 Mar 2024 – 01 Mar 2029**; issued volume shown on registry page; storage-site locations reported as secured upon offtake execution.
- **Company narrative:** partners with farmers for water-stressed soils / productivity co-benefits; first Isometric CCP-labeled biochar credits ([Charm blog](https://charmindustrial.com/blog/charm-verified-biochar-credits)).
- Soil storage module reference: https://registry.isometric.com/module/biochar-storage-soil-environments/1.3

### Verra Project 4679 — “Reignite,” Odisha, India (VM0044)

- First project registered under **VM0044** (announced **8 Oct 2024**).
- Rural farmers convert agricultural waste (instead of open burning) to biochar used **on the same farms as a soil amendment** ([Verra news](https://verra.org/verra-registers-first-biochar-project/)).
- Methodology page: https://verra.org/methodologies/vm0044-biochar-utilization-in-soil-and-non-soil-applications-v1-2/

## Commercial farms / vineyards (US)

### Oasis Vineyard Trial — Monterey County, California

- Partners: Sonoma Ecology Center, **UC Riverside**, Monterey Pacific Inc., **Pacific Biochar**; funded via California Department of Water Resources.
- Pre-plant (2016–2017): biochar **10 t/ac**, compost **15 t/ac**, combo, vs control on sandy low-OM soils; incorporation ~30" in vine row.
- **Five harvests:** combo **+36%**, biochar **+25%**, compost **+29%** average yield vs control; positive ROI by second harvest; incremental revenue by fifth harvest **$9,752 / $12,569 / $14,937** per acre (biochar / compost / combo) at ~$1,500/ton grapes; amendment costs (2016 delivered) biochar **$240/ton**, compost **$40/ton** wet.
- Primary source PDF: https://pacificbiochar.com/wp-content/uploads/Oasis-Vineyard-Trial_-5th-harvest_-Final-Draft_12.12.2023.pdf · [[Oasis Vineyard Trial 2023]]

### Dominus Estate — Napa Valley, California

- Pacific Biochar case study: **25 t/ac** biochar cultivated to **36"** on a **6-acre** vineyard redevelopment after fallow; SOM-equivalent design discussion; earthworks fall/winter 2022, profile pit summer 2023.
- URL: https://pacificbiochar.com/case-study-biochar-application-with-dominus-estate-in-napa-valley-after-2-years-fallow/

### Sonoma Ecology Center × Napa Recycling — American Canyon pyrolysis (startup 2025)

- **23 Mar 2025** startup of CAL FIRE / Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund–supported facility converting surplus **urban woodchips** (A Plus Tree feedstock) to biochar at Napa Recycling; plan to **blend with Napa Recycling compost** for farm/garden customers ([Sonoma Biochar Initiative, 17 May 2025](https://sonomabiocharinitiative.org/sec-and-napa-recycling-pyrolysis-startup/)).
- Related SEC biochar program pages: https://sonomaecologycenter.org/biochar/

### Earth Foundries × Napa Recycling — composting with biochar

- 2023 project report: **10% (by volume) biochar** addition to green/food-waste composting reduced **VOCs 33%**, accelerated curing, affected finished compost nutrient/salinity metrics ([PDF](https://www.earthfoundriesinc.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Earth-Foundries-Green-Compost-Biochar-Composting-Project-Report-2023.pdf) · [[Earth Foundries Co-Composting Report 2023]]).

## How to read these cases

1. **Credit project ≠ agronomic trial.** Exomad/Charm/Verra prove soil **sink pathways** under MRV; yield numbers need separate trial reports.
2. **Vineyard California cases** are among the best public multi-year agronomic + economic datasets (Oasis PDF).
3. **Composting co-benefits** (VOC, curing) are facility metrics—do not equate to field N₂O ([[GHG Fluxes]]).
4. Always check feedstock (forest residue, urban wood, ag waste) against local residue-retention rules ([[NRCS CPS 336 and US Ag]]).
