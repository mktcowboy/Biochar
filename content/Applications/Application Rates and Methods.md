# Application Rates and Methods

Practice-oriented guide to **how much** biochar to apply and **how** to place it. Pair with [[Soil and Agriculture]], [[Biochar Inoculation for Fertilizer]], [[Biochar Compost and Manure Blends]], [[NRCS CPS 336 and US Ag]], and [[Agronomic Economics]]. Every rate claim below is sourced.

## Principles

1. **Soil- and product-specific.** NRCS: rates should be based on soil tests **and** carbon-amendment lab results; biochar properties vary widely ([CPS 336 FAQ, Apr 2026](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).
2. **Charge / inoculate** fresh char with compost, manure, compost tea, or nutrients before or during application when agronomic response is a goal ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf); [CPS 336 2022 PDF](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2022-11/336-NHCP-CPS-Soil-Carbon-Amendment-2022.pdf)).
3. **Incorporate** when possible; surface-only applications risk wind/water loss, especially on slopes ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).
4. Separate **CDR mass** goals from **agronomic optimum**—high research rates are not always economic ([[Agronomic Economics]]).

## Charging / inoculation before rate setting (critical)

Bulk rate tables below assume you have already decided **how** the char is conditioned. For agronomic response, treat charging as part of the rate decision:

### Unit conversion caveats

NRCS FAQ: dry weight of **1 cubic yard** of biochar ranges roughly **135–540 lb** depending on material and moisture ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)). Always convert with **measured bulk density and moisture** from the spec sheet—do not assume a universal t/ha ↔ yd³/ac factor.

## Methods of placement

### Broadcast + incorporation

Standard manure/lime spreaders followed by tillage, disc, or rotary incorporation. NRCS: incorporate “the same ways soil amendments are normally incorporated” including tillage, trenching, no-till drill, permanent beds, surface apply, or mixed into water; **best results** with incorporation ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).

### Banding / seed-row / planting-row

Concentrate char where roots explore. Oasis Vineyard used GPS-ripped delve down the vine row, mixed with winged plow to **~30"** in a bowl ~**2 ft wide × 2.5 ft deep** ([Oasis PDF](https://pacificbiochar.com/wp-content/uploads/Oasis-Vineyard-Trial_-5th-harvest_-Final-Draft_12.12.2023.pdf)). Exomad describes planting-row incorporation and trenching for perennials ([Exomad](https://www.exomadgreen.com/biochar)). Low-dose root-zone enriched chars in Nepal used localized placement ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/agriculture5030723)).

### Deep incorporation / redevelopment

Dominus Estate: excavator / earthworks cultivating biochar to **36"** after fallow redevelopment ([case study](https://pacificbiochar.com/case-study-biochar-application-with-dominus-estate-in-napa-valley-after-2-years-fallow/)). NRCS notes deep tillage is a **one-time** disturbance relative to multi-century char C ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).

### No-till / drill / prills / injection

Crush/screen or compress to prills for drills; inject biochar–manure mixes; fertigate micronized suspensions. Plugging risk if particle size mismatches equipment ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)). Exomad lists liquid injection / fertigation for perennials ([Exomad](https://www.exomadgreen.com/biochar)).

### Pasture topdress

CPS 336 may be planned on **pasture**; **not** on native rangeland except to facilitate CPS 550 or 342. Surface topdress without incorporation increases loss risk—follow slope/runoff restrictions ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)). Brazil pasture research example: [Latawiec et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-47647-x).

### Composting facility co-batching

Add biochar **into** composting (not only field blend). Earth Foundries × Napa Recycling: **10% biochar** in green/food compost reduced VOCs **33%** and shortened curing ([PDF](https://www.earthfoundriesinc.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Earth-Foundries-Green-Compost-Biochar-Composting-Project-Report-2023.pdf)). SEC/Napa Recycling American Canyon pathway blends new pyrolysis char with compost for farm/garden sales ([SBI May 2025](https://sonomabiocharinitiative.org/sec-and-napa-recycling-pyrolysis-startup/)). See [[Biochar Compost and Manure Blends]].

## Timing

NRCS FAQ: apply when ground is not frozen; common timing is **early spring** or **post-harvest with cover crop** so char equilibrates and erosion/nutrient loss is reduced; combining with other organic amendments or planned tillage improves efficiency ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).

## Quality gates before rate setting

From CPS 336 Table 3 / FAQ reading guide ([2022 PDF](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2022-11/336-NHCP-CPS-Soil-Carbon-Amendment-2022.pdf); [FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)):

- Production **>350 °C**, limited oxygen (pyrolysis/gasification)
- **C_org > 10%** DW; **H:C_org < 0.7**
- Metals within Table 3 maxima; report ash, liming equivalent, EC, nutrients
- Prefer recent batch tests; IBI seal alone is **not** sufficient without spec sheet
- **No biosolids** feedstocks under CPS 336 (PFAS)

USBI application fact sheet: https://biochar-us.org/sites/default/files/learning/files/USBI_FACT_SHEET_DFB_Application_Guide_01.pdf  
NRCS calculator / tools landing: https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/conservation-basics/natural-resource-concerns/soil/soil-health/soil-carbon-amendments

## Contracting lifespan (US)

CPS 336 practice lifespan **1 year**; may be contracted up to **5 times** over a contract life. Ranking improves when stacked with other soil-health practices (cover crops, no-till, rotations, etc.) ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).
