## Principles

1. **Soil- and product-specific.** NRCS: rates should be based on soil tests **and** carbon-amendment lab results ([CPS 336 FAQ, Apr 2026](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf) · [[Soil Carbon Amendment FAQ|answers CPS 336 planning questions]]).
2. **Charge / inoculate** fresh char with compost, manure, compost tea, or nutrients before or during application when agronomic response is a goal ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf); [CPS 336 2022 PDF](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2022-11/336-NHCP-CPS-Soil-Carbon-Amendment-2022.pdf) · [[Soil Carbon Amendment Standard|defines NRCS practice 336]]).
3. **Incorporate** when possible; surface-only applications risk wind/water loss, especially on slopes ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).
4. Separate **CDR mass** goals from **agronomic optimum**—high research rates are not always economic ([[Agronomic Economics]], [[Matrix Pathways for Credits]]).

### Unit conversion caveats

NRCS FAQ: dry weight of **1 cubic yard** of biochar ranges roughly **135–540 lb** ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)). Always convert with **measured bulk density and moisture**.

## Methods of placement (overview)

Detail: [[Incorporation Topdress Banding]].

- **Broadcast + incorporation** — manure/lime spreaders + tillage ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).
- **Banding / seed-row** — Oasis GPS rip + winged plow bowl ([Oasis PDF](https://pacificbiochar.com/wp-content/uploads/Oasis-Vineyard-Trial_-5th-harvest_-Final-Draft_12.12.2023.pdf)); Nepal root-zone ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/agriculture5030723)).
- **Deep incorporation** — Dominus to 36" ([case](https://pacificbiochar.com/case-study-biochar-application-with-dominus-estate-in-napa-valley-after-2-years-fallow/)).
- **No-till / drill / prills / injection / fertigation** — FAQ plugging caution; Exomad fertigation ([Exomad](https://www.exomadgreen.com/biochar)).
- **Pasture topdress** — CPS 336 pasture OK; not native rangeland except facilitating CPS 550/342 ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).
- **Composting co-batch** — [[Farm Co-composting Practice]].

## Quality gates before rate setting

From CPS 336 Table 3 / FAQ ([2022 PDF](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2022-11/336-NHCP-CPS-Soil-Carbon-Amendment-2022.pdf); [FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)):

- Production **>350 °C**, limited oxygen (pyrolysis/gasification)
- **C_org > 10%** DW; **H:C_org < 0.7**
- Metals within Table 3 maxima; report ash, liming equivalent, EC, nutrients
- Prefer recent batch tests; IBI seal alone is **not** sufficient without spec sheet
- **No biosolids** feedstocks under CPS 336 (PFAS)

USBI/ABI application guidelines (2026): https://biochar.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Biochar-Guidelines-for-Agriculture-Applications-2026.pdf · [[USBI Guidance vs Vault Notes]] · [[US Biochar Initiative]]

## Contracting lifespan (US)

CPS 336 practice lifespan **1 year**; may be contracted up to **5 times** over a contract life. Ranking improves when stacked with other soil-health practices ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).
