# Fertilizer Displacement

When biochar, blends, or biochar-based fertilizers (BBFs) **change fertilizer need or fertilizer-use efficiency**—sourced evidence only. Parent: [[Agronomic Economics]]. Chemistry: [[Biochar Nutrients and Fertilizer Chemistry]]. Practice: [[Biochar Inoculation for Fertilizer]].

Report context: **2026-09-16**. This note does **not** invent $/ac fertilizer savings without a cited trial that measured them.

---

## 1. What “displacement” can mean

| Meaning | Evidence type | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| Higher yield **at same fertilizer rate** | Field metas / trials | Not the same as cutting fertilizer |
| Equal yield **at lower fertilizer rate** | Explicit N/P rate factorials | Rare in public farm demos |
| Nutrient **carrier** replacing some soluble fertilizer mass | BBF / urine-char / co-compost products | Still a nutrient input—track total NPK |
| Reduced **losses** (leaching, NH₃) improving effective fertilizer | Process and soil studies | May not show on fertilizer invoice |

Oasis Vineyard explicitly held irrigation and fertilizer **constant** across treatments—so its revenue gains are **not** fertilizer-displacement dollars ([Oasis PDF](https://pacificbiochar.com/wp-content/uploads/Oasis-Vineyard-Trial_-5th-harvest_-Final-Draft_12.12.2023.pdf) · [[Oasis Vineyard Trial 2023]]).

## 3. Practical budgeting rules

1. Credit fertilizer savings **only** if the trial measured reduced fertilizer or equal yield at lower NPK.
2. When using co-compost or manure blends, count **compost/manure NPK** toward the nutrient budget ([[Compost Blend Safety]], P-index).
3. Prefer **banding + enrichment** when the goal is cutting total biochar tonnes while maintaining root-zone nutrients ([[Incorporation Topdress Banding]], [[Field Rates by System]]).
4. NRCS still expects nutrient management plans when manure inoculates char ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).
