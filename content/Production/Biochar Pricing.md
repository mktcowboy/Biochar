# Biochar Pricing

Bulk and retail **product** prices for biochar (soil amendment / compost use)—**not** CDR credit prices. Snapshot compiled **2026-09-21** for **Goldsby / Norman, Oklahoma** US-bulk context. All figures below are copied from the source comparison file; **do not invent prices**. Conversions and density assumptions are marked († ‡ §).

**Related notes:** [[Biochar vs Activated Carbon]] · [[Cost Components]] · [[Agronomic Economics]] · [[Market Volumes and Pricing]] · [[Price Indexes and Benchmarks]] · [[Production Hub]] · [[Key Sources]]

---

## Quick summary

US bulk biochar commonly lands in roughly **$200–$650/ton** for soil-product grades in the ranked market/retail table below (midpoints of listed ranges). Specialty totes, inoculated yard tiers, and small bags run much higher. Production **cost** benchmarks are tabulated separately and are **not** selling prices.

Biochar is typically cheaper than activated carbon because it skips steam/chemical activation—see [[Biochar vs Activated Carbon]] for the materials contrast (product $/ton detail stays here).

## How this differs from CDR credit prices

This note is **product $/short ton** (or converted $/ton) for the solid. Credit markets quote **$/tCO₂e** for removals (CORCCHAR / CDR.fyi)—see [[Market Volumes and Pricing]] and [[Price Indexes and Benchmarks]] (CORCCHAR). Do not mix the units in a farm or offtake budget ([[Cost Components]]).
