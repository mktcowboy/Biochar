# Contaminants and Quality

Hub for **environmental contaminants and product-quality framing** of biochar: PAHs, heavy metals/ash, PFAS—including whole-process transfer/destruction and soil immobilization—and the testing stack (EBC / IBI / ANSI S668). Not a carbon-credit methodology. Report context: **2026-09-16**.

## 1. Why quality control matters

Biochar’s climate and soil value collapses if products introduce **PAHs**, **heavy metals**, **dioxins/PCBs**, or **PFAS** into soils, feed, or materials beyond regulatory and certification limits. Consensus in the science report and EBC practice: **clean process design + batch testing** against recognized limits ([EBC](https://www.european-biochar.org/); [IBI](https://biochar-international.org/); science report §3.4). Market-side safeguards also appear in [[MRV Stack]] and [[Risks and Controversies]].

## 3. Standards layers (do not confuse)

| Layer | Examples | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Product standards** | [EBC](https://www.european-biochar.org/) / [IBI](https://biochar-international.org/) | Pass/fail material classes, H/C_org, contaminant limits |
| **Test methods (U.S.)** | [[ANSI ASABE USBI S668]] · [PDF](https://biochar.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ANSI-S668.pdf) | How to measure (incl. PFAS EPA 1633A)—**not** pass/fail by itself |
| **Carbon methodologies** | Verra / Puro / Isometric / CSI | May **require** EBC/IBI or equivalent characterization |
| **Farm practice** | [[NRCS CPS 336 and US Ag]] | Eligibility, not tradable credits |

CSI Global Biochar C-Sink requires **EBC- or WBC-certified** biochar—see [[CSI Global Biochar C-Sink]]. Detail: [[Testing EBC IBI S668]] · [[Product Standards EBC IBI]].

## 4. Elemental-ratio quality gates (reminder)

- H/C_org ≤ **0.7** (EBC/IBI/IUPAC biochar definition); FeedPlus / manure **< 0.4** ([EBC 10.5E](https://www.european-biochar.org/media/doc/7/ebc_en_10_5_red.pdf); [[Bilias et al. 2024]] · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1515/pac-2021-0106))
- O/C_org often / should be ≤ **0.4** ([[Properties]] · [[H-Corg and O-Corg]])

These are **quality/definition** gates; permanence factors are separate ([[Permanence]]).

## 5. Headline EBC contaminant numbers (v10.5E)

From [EBC Guidelines 10.5E (14 Aug 2025)](https://www.european-biochar.org/media/doc/7/ebc_en_10_5_red.pdf):

| Parameter | FeedPlus / Agro / AgroOrganic (indicative) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Σ16 EPA PAH | **6.0 ± 2.4 mg kg⁻¹ DM** | Analytical accuracy caveat in EBC text |
| Σ8 EFSA PAH | **1.0 mg kg⁻¹ DM** (also Urban/Materials) | Basic allows **4** mg kg⁻¹ Σ8 |
| Heavy metals | Class-specific Table 2 (Pb, Cd, Cu, Ni, Hg, Zn, Cr, As) | Basic: declaration only |
| PCB / PCDD/F | Triggered if Σ8 EFSA PAH > 1 mg kg⁻¹ | Limits 0.2 mg kg⁻¹ PCB; 20 ng kg⁻¹ I-TEQ PCDD/F |

Full discussion: [[PAHs]] · [[Heavy Metals and Ash]] · [[Testing EBC IBI S668]].

## 6. Practice takeaway

1. Design reactors for **vapor–char separation before cooling**; then test every batch.
2. Match **certification class** to end use (feed ≠ materials ≠ basic industrial).
3. For biosolids, coated-paper/compost, or impacted-waste routes, budget **metals + PFAS** analytics and whole-train sampling up front ([[PFAS]]).
4. Use S668/EPA methods in North America; EBC-endorsed labs for EBC PAH methods (matrix matters).
