# Quantitative Benchmarks

Cross-source cheat sheet. Each figure's checked home is its source note (index in [[Key Sources]]); if a number here disagrees with a source note, the source note wins. Do not invent figures beyond these sources.

|---|---|

## Carbon fraction (Woolf 2021 examples)

- Wood pyrolysis mean FC ≈ **0.76**; high-T wood ≈ **0.81** ([Woolf et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.1c02425))
- Manure ≈ **0.39**; sewage sludge ≈ **0.37**
- Gasification sludge/manure ~**0.10–0.14**

## Application rates

- Research: **~5–50+ t ha⁻¹** (sometimes 100; [Jeffery 2011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agee.2011.08.015) extremes)
- Nutrient-enriched fertilizers: **<2 t ha⁻¹**
- [Woolf 2021](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.1c02425) N₂O credit: only **>10 Mg C ha⁻¹** year 1
