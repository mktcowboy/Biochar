# Recent Research 2021–2026

## Major reviews & methods (climate / permanence / accounting)

1. **[[Lehmann et al. 2021|Lehmann et al. (2021)]].** Biochar in climate change mitigation. *Nature Geoscience* 14:883–892. DOI: [10.1038/s41561-021-00852-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41561-021-00852-8). Global system potential **3.4–6.3 Pg CO₂e yr⁻¹** emission reductions (~half CDR); with land sparing/CCS pathways up to **~7.4–10.3 Pg CO₂e yr⁻¹**. Emphasizes H/C_org, feedstock sustainability, energy vs sequestration trade-offs.

2. **[[Woolf et al. 2021|Woolf et al. (2021)]].** Greenhouse Gas Inventory Model for Biochar Additions to Soil. *Environ. Sci. Technol.* 55:14795–14805. DOI: [10.1021/acs.est.1c02425](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.1c02425). ~183 citations (S2). Operational EF model extending IPCC 2019; F_perm from T class or H/C_org; temperature-adjusted.

3. **IPCC (2019).** *2019 Refinement…* Vol. 4 Appendix 4. [PDF](https://www.ipcc-nggip.iges.or.jp/public/2019rf/pdf/4_Volume4/19R_V4_Ch02_Ap4_Biochar.pdf) · [[IPCC 2019 Biochar Appendix]]. F_perm after 100 yr: **0.65 / 0.80 / 0.89** for low / medium / high T.

4. **[[Bilias et al. 2024|Bilias et al. (2024)]].** Glossary of terms used in biochar research (IUPAC Technical Report). *Pure Appl. Chem.* DOI: [10.1515/pac-2021-0106](https://doi.org/10.1515/pac-2021-0106). Formal terminology (178 terms).

5. **Weng & Cowie (2025).** Estimates vary but credible evidence points to gigaton-scale… *Commun. Earth Environ.* DOI: [10.1038/s43247-025-02228-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43247-025-02228-x). Literature range **~0.03–11 Pg CO₂e yr⁻¹**; credible centering around multi-Gt scale.

6. **[[Fuss & Lunstrum 2026|Fuss & Lunstrum (2026)]].** Chapter 10: Costs and potentials. In *The State of Carbon Dioxide Removal*, 3rd ed. DOI: [10.17605/OSF.IO/FBZ4X](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/FBZ4X). Sustainable removal up to **~2.7 GtCO₂ yr⁻¹**; total mitigation up to **~10.3 GtCO₂ yr⁻¹**; IAM Highest Ambition **<0.1–1.1 GtCO₂ yr⁻¹ by 2050**; costs often **US$70–360/tCO₂**.

7. **Schmidt et al. (2025).** Biochar Permanence—A Policy Commentary. *GCB Bioenergy*. DOI: [10.1111/gcbb.70092](https://doi.org/10.1111/gcbb.70092). Centuries–millennia for condensed fractions; multi-proxy discussion.

## Soil amendments, crop yields, GHG fluxes

8. **[[Jia et al. 2023|Jia et al. (2023)]].** Global patterns… soil GHG fluxes and crop yield. *Land Degradation & Development*. DOI: [10.1002/ldr.4868](https://doi.org/10.1002/ldr.4868). 3883 paired obs / 220 studies: yield **+9.4%**; CH₄ **−13.0%**; N₂O **−26.8%**; GWP **−7.7%**.

9. **Global field vs lab synthesis (2022).** *Renewable & Sustainable Energy Reviews*. Field: yield **+15.7%**, CH₄ **−14.8%**, N₂O **−23.1%**, SOC stocks **+26.6%**.

10. **[[Jeffery et al. 2017|Jeffery et al. (2017)]].** Biochar boosts tropical but not temperate crop yields. *Environ. Res. Lett.* 12:053001. DOI: [10.1088/1748-9326/aa67bd](https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/aa67bd) — still widely cited.

11. **[[Ye et al. 2020|Ye et al. (2020)]].** Biochar effects on crop yields with and without fertilizer… *Soil Use Manage.* 36:2–18 — used heavily in Lehmann 2021.

12. **Weng et al. (2017).** Biochar built soil carbon over a decade… *Nature Climate Change* 7:371–376. DOI: [10.1038/nclimate3276](https://doi.org/10.1038/nclimate3276). ~344 citations — decade-scale negative priming / rhizodeposit stabilization.

13. **Blanco-Canqui et al. (2020).** Soil carbon increased by twice the amount of biochar carbon applied after 6 years… *GCB Bioenergy* 12:240–251.

## Contaminants / process

PAH content depends on feedstock, T, vapor residence, and especially post-pyrolysis vapor condensation. Clean process design + batch testing against EBC/IBI limits is the practical consensus. See [[Contaminants and Quality]]; product limits: [EBC](https://www.european-biochar.org/) / [IBI](https://biochar-international.org/).
