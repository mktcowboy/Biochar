# Properties

| Property | Why it matters | Typical science notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Stable / aromatic C** | Persistence & CDR | Fused aromatic rings increase with T and time; H/C_org and O/C_org track condensation |
| **Porosity / surface area** | Water, nutrients, microbes, sorption | Increases generally with higher pyrolysis T (then can decline at very high T) |
| **pH / liming** | Acid soils, Al toxicity | Many lignocellulosic biochars are alkaline |
| **H/C_org** | Persistence proxy; EBC/IBI quality | Upper limit **0.7** for “biochar”; lower ≈ more condensed |
| **O/C_org** | Persistence proxy (Spokas) | Often **<0.4** required; lower ≈ longer expected half-life |
| **CEC / ash / nutrients** | Agronomy | Ash-rich chars supply minerals; CEC varies widely |

## Quality thresholds (standards)

- **EBC/IBI:** H/C_org ≤ 0.7 (sometimes stricter for feed grades); O/C_org often ≤ 0.4
- Molecular transitions with charring temperature: Keiluweit et al. (2010), *Environ. Sci. Technol.* DOI: [10.1021/es9031419](https://doi.org/10.1021/es9031419)
- O/C as half-life predictor: Spokas (2010), *Carbon Management* DOI: [10.4155/cmt.10.32](https://doi.org/10.4155/cmt.10.32)
