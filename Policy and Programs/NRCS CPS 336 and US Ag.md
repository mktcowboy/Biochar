# NRCS CPS 336 and US Ag

USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service **Conservation Practice Standard 336 — Soil Carbon Amendment**: EQIP/CSP (and related) **cost-share for applying biochar and/or compost** to improve soil health and store carbon. This is a **conservation practice standard**, **not** a carbon-credit methodology. Pair with [[Soil and Agriculture]], [[Application Rates and Methods]], [[Biochar Compost and Manure Blends]], [[Double Counting and Credit Ownership]].

## What CPS 336 is for

Stated purposes include improve/maintain SOM, sequester/enhance soil C stocks, improve aggregate stability, and improve habitat for soil organisms; producers may also seek moisture and productivity co-benefits ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)). Sandy, acidic, less-healthy soils are called out as more likely to respond.

## Biochar eligibility specs (practice)

From national standard Table 3 / narrative ([2022 PDF](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2022-11/336-NHCP-CPS-Soil-Carbon-Amendment-2022.pdf)):

- Produced **>350 °C** under limited oxygen (pyrolysis or gasification)
- **Organic carbon (C_org) > 10%** dry weight
- **H:C_org < 0.7** (molar) — lower preferred for sequestration
- Metals and pathogens within Table limits; report ash, liming equivalent, nutrients, etc.
- IBI Certified seal **or** documented Table 3 methods — but FAQ: certificate alone is **not** enough; planner still needs the **spec sheet** ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf))

### Explicit exclusions / restrictions

- **No biosolids / municipal waste** biochar or compost under 336 (PFAS and related concerns) ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf))
- Residues that should remain for soil cover or forest health cannot be diverted into paid 336 feedstock pathways ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf))
- Humic/fulvic products are **not** stand-alone carbon amendments under 336 (may be inoculants) ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf))

## Planning rates and FY2024 scenarios

FAQ: **4–10 cubic yards per acre** is a general starting range; characteristics of soil and amendment drive the final rate ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).

| FY2024 scenario (FAQ Table 1) | Compost | Biochar |
|---|---|---|
| 100% Biochar (acres unit) | — | **4 yd³/ac** |
| 20% biochar / 80% compost | 4.4 t/ac | 0.8 yd³/ac |
| 40% biochar / 60% compost | 3.3 t/ac | 1.6 yd³/ac |
| 60% biochar / 40% compost | 2.2 t/ac | 2.4 yd³/ac |
| 80% biochar / 20% compost | 1.1 t/ac | 3.2 yd³/ac |
| Compost off-site | 3 t/ac | — |

Cubic-yard and small-area (ft²) scenarios exist for non-standard rates. Dry weight of 1 yd³ biochar ≈ **135–540 lb** ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)).

Payment construction example: FY2023 **60/40** blend scenario ~**$160/yd³** average-cost build (state-varying; not invoice-matched) ([Farmland Info guide](https://farmlandinfo.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2023/12/biochar_funding_for_US_producers_quick_guide_.pdf)).

## Application practice (NRCS)

- Incorporate when possible (tillage, trench, drill, beds, fertigation); surface-only risks wind/water loss ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf))
- Inoculate with compost, compost tea, or manure ([2022 PDF](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2022-11/336-NHCP-CPS-Soil-Carbon-Amendment-2022.pdf))
- Pasture OK; **native rangeland** only if facilitating CPS 550 or 342 ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf))
- Lifespan **1 year**; up to **5** applications over a contract ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf))
- Ranking improves when bundled with other soil-health practices (cover crops, no-till, rotations, etc.) ([FAQ](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf))

## Ag pathway checklist (operator-oriented)

1. **Feedstock:** Prefer documented wastes/residues; respect residue-retention rules.
2. **Produce & test:** Meet CPS 336 Table 3 / EBC-WBC / IBI-style specs; target low H:C_org for CDR eligibility.
3. **Choose registry early** if also selling credits: [[Verra VM0044]] · [[Puro CORC200+]] · [[Isometric Biochar Protocol]] · [[CSI Global Biochar C-Sink]].
4. **Secure offtake & credit ownership** in farmer / applicator contracts.
5. **Stack carefully:** NRCS payments ≠ carbon credits; prevent double claiming ([[Double Counting and Credit Ownership]]).
6. **Apply:** Incorporate; watch pH, EC, P loading, slope; prefer compost blends ([[Biochar Compost and Manure Blends]]).
7. **MRV:** Batch IDs, weigh tickets, geotagged application, multi-year records ([[MRV Stack]]).
