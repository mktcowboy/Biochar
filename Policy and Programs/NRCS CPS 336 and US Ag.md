# NRCS CPS 336 and US Ag

## NRCS CPS 336 — Soil Carbon Amendment

- **FAQ (April 2026):** [PDF](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2026-04/soil-carbon-amendment-ac-336-faq.pdf)
- EQIP/CSP **cost-share practice**—**not** a carbon credit methodology
- Spec sheet: H:C_org **max 0.7** for practice eligibility
- Typical planning rates ~**4–10 yd³/ac**
- Can coexist with CDR credits only if double-counting is contractually prevented—see [[Double Counting and Credit Ownership]]
- Excludes biosolids chars over PFAS concerns

## Ag pathway checklist (operator-oriented)

1. **Feedstock:** Prefer true wastes/residues with documented counterfactual; avoid stripping residues needed for soil cover unless justified.
2. **Produce & test:** Meet EBC/WBC or at least IBI-style spec; target low H/C_org for CDR eligibility.
3. **Choose registry early:** VM0044 vs Puro vs Isometric vs CSI differ on waste rules, new-facility rules, permanence math, and application evidence.
4. **Secure offtake & credit ownership** in farmer / applicator contracts.
5. **Stack carefully:** NRCS CPS 336 payments ≠ carbon credits; disclose and prevent double claiming.
6. **Application:** Incorporate where possible; watch pH, EC, P loading, slope/erosion; consider compost blends.
7. **MRV systems:** Batch IDs, weigh tickets, geotagged application, 5+ year record retention.
