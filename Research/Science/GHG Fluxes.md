# GHG Fluxes

## Summary table

| Gas | Typical cited ranges | Caveats |
|---|---|---|
| **N₂O** | Meta means historically **~−12% to −54%**; recent field-focused ~**−23%**; Woolf 2021 uses **−23%** first year only (>10 Mg C ha⁻¹) | Effect fades / uncertain beyond year 1 |
| **CH₄** | Mixed: paddies sometimes ↓ (East Asia meta **−22.9%**), sometimes ↑ or ns; global metas often **ns overall** | Woolf 2021 assumes **no net CH₄ change** for inventory |
| **CO₂ / priming** | Short-term positive priming common; long-term often **negative priming** (~**−4%** mean SOC mineralization in Wang 2016; field decade-scale accrual in Weng 2017) | Organic/forest soils: do **not** apply Woolf 2021 method |

## Recent field-focused metas

- **Jia et al. (2023):** CH₄ **−13.0%**; N₂O **−26.8%**; GWP **−7.7%**; field CO₂ often ns. DOI: [10.1002/ldr.4868](https://doi.org/10.1002/ldr.4868)
- **Field synthesis 2022:** CH₄ **−14.8%**, N₂O **−23.1%**, SOC stocks **+26.6%**

## Classic anchors

- Cayuela et al. (2014) — classic N₂O meta (~54% in that analysis; later field work more conservative). DOI: [10.1016/j.agee.2013.10.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agee.2013.10.009)
- Wang et al. (2016) — decomposition + priming. DOI: [10.1111/gcbb.12266](https://doi.org/10.1111/gcbb.12266)
- Weng et al. (2017) — decade-scale negative priming. DOI: [10.1038/nclimate3276](https://doi.org/10.1038/nclimate3276)
- Blanco-Canqui et al. (2020) — field negative priming evidence

## Inventory note

Woolf et al. (2021) optional N₂O credit only for applications **>10 Mg C ha⁻¹** in first year (conservative inventory choice). DOI: [10.1021/acs.est.1c02425](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.1c02425)
