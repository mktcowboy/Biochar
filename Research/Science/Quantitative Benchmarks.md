# Quantitative Benchmarks

Single source of truth for numbers cited from the science report. Do not invent figures beyond these sources.

## Carbon permanence (F_perm / % remaining)

| Source | Finding |
|---|---|
| IPCC 2019 App. 4 | 100-yr F_perm: **0.65 / 0.80 / 0.89** (low/med/high T) |
| Woolf et al. 2021 | At 14.9 °C: 100-yr ≈ **0.63 / 0.71 / 0.82**; 1000-yr high-T ~0.25 |
| Lehmann et al. 2021 | H/C_org < 0.5 subset: mean **~82%** remaining at 100 yr (95% of cases >50%) |
| EBC/IBI | H/C_org ≤ **0.7**; O/C_org often ≤ **0.4** |

## Crop yields

| Source | Effect |
|---|---|
| Jeffery 2011 | Mean **~+10%** (−28% to +39%) |
| Jeffery 2017 | Stronger in tropics |
| Jia 2023 | **+9.4%** |
| Field synthesis 2022 | **+15.7%** |

## GHG fluxes

| Gas | Benchmark |
|---|---|
| N₂O | Historical metas ~−12% to −54%; recent field ~**−23%** to **−26.8%** (Jia 2023) |
| CH₄ | Jia 2023 **−13.0%**; mixed/paddies study-dependent |
| Priming | Wang 2016 mean SOC mineralization ~**−4%**; Weng 2017 decade-scale accrual |

## CDR / mitigation (Gt or Pg CO₂e yr⁻¹)

| Source | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Lehmann 2021 | **3.4–6.3** (up to **7.4–10.3** with pathways) |
| Weng & Cowie 2025 | Literature **0.03–11**; multi-Gt credible |
| State of CDR 2026 | Removal ≤**~2.7**; total mitigation ≤**~10.3**; IAM 2050 **<0.1–1.1** |
| Costs | **~$70–360/tCO₂** (recent) |

## Carbon fraction (Woolf 2021 examples)

- Wood pyrolysis mean FC ≈ **0.76**; high-T wood ≈ **0.81**
- Manure ≈ **0.39**; sewage sludge ≈ **0.37**
- Gasification sludge/manure ~**0.10–0.14**

## Application rates

- Research: **~5–50+ t ha⁻¹** (sometimes 100)
- Nutrient-enriched fertilizers: **<2 t ha⁻¹**
- Woolf 2021 N₂O credit: only **>10 Mg C ha⁻¹** year 1
