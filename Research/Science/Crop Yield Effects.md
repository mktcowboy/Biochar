# Crop Yield Effects

Yield responses are **context-specific**—stronger in acid, degraded, and tropical soils; not a universal fertilizer substitute.

## Meta-analytic estimates

| Source | Effect |
|---|---|
| Jeffery et al. 2011 | Mean **~+10%**; range roughly **−28% to +39%** |
| Jeffery et al. 2017 | Stronger in **tropics** than temperate |
| Ye et al. 2020 / Lehmann 2021 | Field fertilized contexts ~**+15%** magnitude comparable to fertilizer response at same sites, but **not correlated** 1:1 with fertilizer response |
| Jia et al. 2023 | **+9.4%** overall |
| Field synthesis 2022 | **+15.7%** field |

## Application rates (research context)

- Historical trials: **~5–50+ t ha⁻¹**, sometimes **100 t ha⁻¹** (Jeffery 2011 extreme)—**not** a general farm recommendation.
- Agronomic / biochar-based fertilizers can work at **<2 t ha⁻¹** when nutrient-enriched (Schmidt et al. Nepal trials, cited in Lehmann 2021).
