# Buyers and Offtakes

## Buyers (illustrative, from CDR.fyi)

Microsoft (dominant volume), Google, Frontier consortium buyers, BCG, JPMorgan, Swiss Re, SEB, Nasdaq, Tide Platform, Altitude (CDR financing fund), banks (e.g., TD), and corporates using BCR as “ready-now / lower-cost durable CDR.”

Top purchasers include **Microsoft, Google, BCG, JPMorgan** (~**57%** of BCR purchases combined per Snapshot 2025).

## Suppliers

Highly concentrated—**Exomad Green** historically ~**60%** of BCR tonnes sold since 2022; 2026 large offtakes include Liferaft, Empacar, AMP, and others. Strong delivery footprint in Global South + growing US/EU industrial capacity.

## Quality tiers (practical buyer framing)

| Tier | Typical claim | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Durable engineered CDR** | Multi-century to millennial storage; ex-post issuance; rigorous LCA | Isometric 200y/1000y; Puro CORC200+; CSI GPC; Verra VM0044 CCP-labeled VCUs |
| **Temporary / semi-persistent** | Time-bounded CINK metrics; methane offsetting | CSI SPC / CINK_H products |
| **Conventional VCM offsets** | Avoidance or short-lived sequestration | Not equivalent to BCR CDR |
| **Uncertified marketing claims** | “Carbon negative” without registry retirement | High greenwashing risk |

## Buyer concentration risk

Microsoft and a few others drive a large share of BCR volume; strategy shifts can shock demand—see [[Risks and Controversies]] and [[Market Volumes and Pricing]].
