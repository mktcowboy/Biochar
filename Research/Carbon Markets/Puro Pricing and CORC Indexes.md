## Deal structures

- Spot from certified inventory
- Multi-year offtakes (firm + optional); report in MyPuro for **retirement-fee waiver** if >1 calendar year delivery span
- Suppliers retain buyer relationships; Puro is certification/registry infrastructure

## Supplier fee model (public)

Source: [puro.earth/certified-carbon-credits/fees](https://puro.earth/certified-carbon-credits/fees/)

- **€1,400**/yr account
- Volume service fee tiers (examples): 1,001–2,000 CORCs → €10.00; mid-tiers step down; ≥9M → €0.40
- ≤1,000 CORC audits → **€12,000 flat** when audit booked
- Retirement **€0.25**; secondary trading **0%**
- Biochar verification-complexity premium **0%**
- 5% discount if service fee paid at issuance
