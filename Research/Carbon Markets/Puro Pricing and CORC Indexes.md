# Puro Pricing and CORC Indexes

**Parent concept:** [[Puro CORC200+]]  
**Operator / registry:** [[Puro.earth Overview]]

Puro is unusual among biochar standards in publishing **public Nasdaq-methodology indexes** of reported CORC transactions. Those indexes are **not** universal quotes for Verra / Isometric / CSI tonnes — treat them as Puro-specific near-term transaction signals.

### How to read them

- Indexes track **reported near-term CORC transactions** under Nasdaq methodology.
- They are a **price discovery** tool for Puro inventory and offtakes — not a clearing price for every BCR tonne globally.
- **CORCCHAR** sits below **CORCX** in the Aug 2026 snapshot (€135.35 vs €150.40), with milder YTD appreciation than the all-CORC index.
- Levels change — **re-pull before investment or offtake decisions**.

## Deal structures

From deep-dive / public Puro materials:

| Structure | Description |
|---|---|
| **Spot** | Certified inventory via Puro platform |
| **Multi-year offtakes** | Firm + optional volumes; retirement-fee waiver if offtake reported in MyPuro and spans **>1 calendar year** |
| **Direct supplier–buyer contracts** | Suppliers keep commercial control; Puro is certification / registry infrastructure, not the exclusive broker of all tonnes |

### Retirement fee waiver (ops detail)

- Standard retirement fee: **€0.25 / CORC**
- Waivable for qualifying multi-year offtakes when reported in MyPuro with delivery spanning more than one calendar year
- Secondary trading fee: **0%**

## Supplier fee model (public)

Source: [puro.earth/certified-carbon-credits/fees](https://puro.earth/certified-carbon-credits/fees/)

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Annual account fee | **€1,400** / 12 months |
| Service fee (volume tiers) | **€10.00 → €0.40** per CORC by trailing 12-month volume |
| Example mid/low tier (deep dive) | 1,001–2,000 CORCs → **€10.00**; ≥9M → **€0.40** |
| Low-volume audits (≤≈1,000 CORC) | **€12,000 flat** when audit booked |
| Biochar verification-complexity premium | **0%** |
| Retirement | **€0.25** / CORC (offtake waiver possible) |
| Secondary trading | **0%** |
| Pooling admin | **€2,000** / facility / year if pooling |
| Issuance-time payment discount | **5%** off service fee if paid at issuance |

### Cost narrative vs verification

- Puro **allocates VVBs** and, per fees page narrative, **covers verification costs**.
- Suppliers pay annual + volume service fees (and retirement unless waived).
- VVB commercial fees are negotiated by Puro (framework or quote); suppliers do not pick auditors from a public shop list.

## Pricing implications for producers (non-advice)

Observations grounded only in report facts:

1. Public **CORCCHAR** discovery lowers information asymmetry vs purely bilateral BCR deals.
2. Volume-tier service fees (**€10 → €0.40**) create strong scale incentives; low-volume audits face the **€12,000 flat** path.
3. Multi-year offtakes can remove the **€0.25** retirement fee if properly reported.
4. Liquidity (22-day mid-2025 cycle time) may matter as much as headline €/t for working-capital planning.
5. Buyers wanting **CCP methodology labels today** may still route demand to [[Verra VM0044]] until Puro biochar is CCP-Approved — a price/segmentation risk, not a fee-table item.

Not legal, accounting, or investment advice. Fees and indexes change.
