Compare [[Puro CORC200+]] with [[Verra VM0044]], [[Isometric Biochar Protocol]], and [[CSI Global Biochar C-Sink]].

## Practical chooser (US producer)

**Prefer Puro** if you want: mature BCR buyer recognition, CORCCHAR price discovery, Nasdaq registry ops, flexible baselines (retrofit/mobile), and can meet end-use evidence.

**Prefer Verra** if buyers require a **CCP-labelled methodology today**, or the project is cleanly a **new waste** facility under VM0044.

**Prefer Isometric** if the offtake demands **1000-year Ro** pathway or maximal protocolized soil MRV.

**Prefer CSI** if you are already **EBC/WBC** certified and want GPC/SPC + CRCF-oriented C-sink tracking.

Avoid claiming the same tonne on multiple registries — see [[Double Counting and Credit Ownership]].
