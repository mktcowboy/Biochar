# Puro CORC200+

**Operator:** Puro.earth (Puro Standard); CORCs in the Puro Registry  
**Current version:** **Biochar Edition 2025** approved **12 June 2025**; **v2** (retail-eligible updates) approved **27 November 2025**

- [Permanence explainer](https://puro.earth/insights/post/biochar-permanence/)
- [CORC indexes](https://puro.earth/buy-carbon-credits/removal-certificate-corc/carbon-removal-indexes/)

## What it credits

Net CO₂ removal from the full biochar supply chain (biomass sourcing → thermochemical conversion → eligible use), issued as **CORC200+**.

## Key rules themes

- Baseline, additionality, leakage/indirect effects, lifecycle emissions, uncertainty reporting, third-party facility & output audits
- Stricter biomass sourcing; enhanced treatment of mobile pyrolyzers
- Eligible use pathways with safeguards; retail-to-individuals (Category R3) made eligible under v2 with product-level risk controls, labelling, contractual clarity, country-level reversal-risk discounts, conservative transport accounting

## Permanence

Shift from CORC100+ (Edition 2022 / Woolf et al. 2021 framing) to **CORC200+** (“several centuries”) with a revised decomposition equation; random reflectance reporting **encouraged** but **does not yet affect quantification** ([Puro, 2025-07-03](https://puro.earth/insights/post/biochar-permanence/)).

## Transition

- New facilities use Edition 2025
- Existing Edition 2022 facilities may continue to end of crediting period; Edition 2022 termination targeted **12 June 2030**
- Facility audits under 2025 from ~**1 Nov 2025**

## Scale note

Puro states it has certified **70+** biochar projects since 2019.
