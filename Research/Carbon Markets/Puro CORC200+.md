## Equation (net CORCs)

\[
\mathrm{CORCs} = C_{\mathrm{stored}} - C_{\mathrm{baseline}} - C_{\mathrm{loss}} - E_{\mathrm{project}} - E_{\mathrm{leakage}}
\]

- \(C_{\mathrm{stored}} = Q_{\mathrm{biochar}} \times C_{\mathrm{org}} \times 44/12\)
- \(C_{\mathrm{loss}}\) from persistence fraction \(PF = M - a \times H/C_{\mathrm{org}}\) (soil-T lookup Table 6.1)
- Details: [[Puro Biochar Methodology Edition 2025]] · [[Puro Project Cycle and MRV]]

## Permanence stance

Puro moved from 100-year Woolf multi-pool decay to a **200-year power-law** model with an **80% CI lower bound**, then assumes deeper-soil protection for further centuries. **Random reflectance (Ro) is encouraged but does not yet change quantification** ([permanence explainer](https://puro.earth/insights/post/biochar-permanence/)). Cross-link: [[Permanence Science for Credits]].
