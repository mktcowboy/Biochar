# Puro.earth Overview

Engineered **carbon dioxide removal (CDR)** standard, registry, and market infrastructure based in Helsinki (Puro.earth Oy). Issues **CO₂ Removal Certificates (CORCs)** — 1 tCO₂e net durable removal each.

## History (sourced)

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 2019 | Fortum internal startup; Original Signatories design standard in ~71 days; first CORC auction **24 May 2019** |
| Jun 2021 | **Nasdaq majority stake**; independent company/brand retained |
| Oct 2021 | Independent **Advisory Board** houses the Puro Standard |
| Dec 2025 | **ICVCM CCP-Eligible** program (General Rules **v4.2+** only) |

Sources: [Fortum 2019](https://www.fortum.com/en/media/2019/04/worlds-first-marketplace-co2-removals-launched-reverse-climate-change), [Nasdaq IR](https://ir.nasdaq.com/news-releases/news-release-details/nasdaq-acquires-emerging-carbon-removal-market-puroearth), [half-decade](https://puro.earth/insights/post/puro-celebrates-half-a-decade/), [ICVCM](https://www.icvcm.org/integrity-council-confirms-carbon-crediting-program-puro-earth-as-ccp-eligible/).

## What makes it different

- **Removal-only** accounting (avoidance not mixed into CORCs)
- Clear durability labels: **100+ / 200+ / 1,000+** by methodology
- Nasdaq-powered **Puro Registry** + public **CORC indexes**
- Puro **allocates VVBs** and (per fees narrative) covers verification costs; suppliers pay volume service fees

- **Program:** CCP-Eligible for facilities under General Rules v4.2+ ([ICVCM 2025-12-11](https://www.icvcm.org/integrity-council-confirms-carbon-crediting-program-puro-earth-as-ccp-eligible/))
