# Applications Hub

Where biochar goes after production: soil and agriculture, greenhouse-gas flux effects, and emerging materials / built-environment uses. Application pathway shapes both agronomic outcomes and how carbon credits treat storage (soil vs concrete/asphalt/composites).

## Domain purpose

Science and markets both care *where* char is placed:

- **Soil** — yield effects, N₂O/CH₄ fluxes, priming, and long-term persistence ([[Permanence]])
- **Built / materials** — locking char into concrete, asphalt, polymers, or other matrices can change monitoring, leakage, and eligibility under credit methods
- **Policy** — US farm practice standards (e.g. NRCS CPS 336) sit alongside voluntary carbon markets

This hub links existing effect notes and holds placeholders for domains not yet researched in depth.
