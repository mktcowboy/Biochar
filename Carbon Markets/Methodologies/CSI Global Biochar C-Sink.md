# CSI Global Biochar C-Sink

**Operator:** Carbon Standards International AG (CSI), with Ithaka Institute (formerly “EBC Biochar C-Sink”)  
**Registry:** [Global C-Sink Registry](https://global-c-registry.org/)  
**Current version:** **Global Biochar C-Sink Standard 3.3** (valid since **9 June 2026**); v3.2 from **15 November 2025**  
**PDF:** [4000039EN.pdf](https://www.carbon-standards.com/docs/transfer/4000039EN.pdf)

## Prerequisite

Only **EBC- or WBC-certified** biochar may receive Global Biochar C-Sink certification. Product class must match use (e.g., EBC-Agro / WBC-Agro for agricultural soil).

## What it credits

A tracked **C-sink** (not avoidance):

| Pool | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **GPC** | Geologically persistent carbon (>1000 years; eligible for CO₂ offsetting under CSI logic) |
| **SPC** | Semi-persistent carbon (decays over ≤1000 years; temporary climate services / methane offsetting) |

## Persistence classes (v3.3)

| Condition | Split |
|---|---|
| H/C_org **> 0.40** | **100% SPC**, 0% GPC |
| H/C_org **≤ 0.40** (lower class) | **75% GPC / 25% SPC** |
| Upper class: mean **Ro ≥ 3.8%** or BC_HyPy **≥ 90%** | **90% GPC / 10% SPC** |

## Emission portfolio

All associated GHG from biomass → application must be registered and **offset** (fossil CO₂/N₂O with GPC; CH₄ with temporary sinks delivering TCE over 5–20 years) **before** C-sink validation.

- Margin of safety **20 kg CO₂e / t biochar** (DM)
- Fossil process emissions must fall below **100 kg CO₂e / t biochar by 2030** and **20 kg by 2035**
- Process energy-use efficiency rule (**>60%** in exec summary); carbon-efficiency / additionality checks
