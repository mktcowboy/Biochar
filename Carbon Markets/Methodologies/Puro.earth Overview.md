# Puro.earth Overview

**Puro.earth** is an engineered **carbon dioxide removal (CDR)** standard, registry, and market-infrastructure company based in Helsinki (**Puro.earth Oy**). It does **not** operate as a conventional multi-sector offset registry: CORCs are framed exclusively as **durable removals**, with avoidance kept out of CORC accounting.

This note is the map of the Puro cluster in the vault. Open child notes for depth; do not treat this overview as a substitute for the methodology or MRV chapters.

## Why it matters for biochar

Biochar carbon removal (BCR) has been one of Puro’s longest-running pathways. Under **[[Puro Biochar Methodology Edition 2025]]**, biochar CORCs are labelled **[[Puro CORC200+|CORC200+]]** (“several centuries,” ≥200 years), with public price discovery via Nasdaq × Puro **[[Puro Pricing and CORC Indexes|CORCCHAR / CORCX]]** indexes. For a US producer choosing among registries, start here, then read [[Puro vs Other Standards]] and [[Puro Project Cycle and MRV]].

## What a CORC is

A **CO₂ Removal Certificate (CORC)** = **1 metric tonne CO₂e net removal**, independently verified under a Puro methodology, recorded in the Puro Registry, transferable, and **retired** when applied to a climate claim ([CORC page](https://puro.earth/buy-carbon-credits/removal-certificate-corc/)).

General marketing still frames “**100+ year durability**” as the baseline CORC definition across pathways. Biochar **Edition 2025** specifically issues **[[Puro CORC200+|CORC200+]]**.

## How Puro fits the CDR / BCR market

1. **Removal-only product.** CORCs are engineered-CDR certificates; they are not a general-purpose offset unit mixing avoidance and removal.
2. **Ex-post issuance after use.** For biochar, the point of CORC creation is the first **durable eligible use**, not the factory gate alone — see [[Puro Project Cycle and MRV]].
3. **Public price signal.** Unlike most BCR pathways, Puro publishes Nasdaq-methodology indexes (**CORCX**, **CORCCHAR**) — see [[Puro Pricing and CORC Indexes]] · [[Market Volumes and Pricing]].
4. **Buyer recognition.** Early Microsoft CORC procurements and CDR.fyi BCR Snapshot 2025 buyer lists (Microsoft, Google, BCG, JPMorgan, Swiss Re, SEB with explicit Puro/CORC narrative) place Puro among the most recognizable BCR venues — see [[Buyers and Offtakes]].
5. **Liquidity trend.** CDR.fyi (citing Puro Aug 2025 report): biochar CORC issuance→transfer/retirement time improved from **95 days (2021)** to **22 days (mid-2025)**.

Broader framing: [[Biochar as Durable CDR]] · [[Carbon Markets Hub]]

## Critiques and open issues (high level)

From the deep-dive research (detail in [[Risks and Controversies]] · [[Puro vs Other Standards]]):

1. **Permanence science divergence** — decay-model camp (CORC200+, Isometric 200y, IPCC/Woolf) vs inertinite/Ro millennial camp (Sanei et al.; Isometric 1000y; CSI upper class). Puro stopped at **centuries**, not millennia, pending further integration ([permanence post](https://puro.earth/insights/post/biochar-permanence/)).
2. **ICVCM two-tick gap** — program eligible, biochar methodology not yet CCP-Approved.
3. **Edition 2022 → 2025 transition** — hard stop **12 Jun 2030**; Preliminary Assessments do not transfer.
4. **No default buffer** — eligibility + supplier liability instead of a pooled % buffer.
5. **Retail / diffuse channels** — higher reversal risk even after R3 v2 safeguards.
6. **Concentration / delivery risk** — BCR market buyer and supplier concentration (CDR.fyi); contracted vs delivered gaps.
