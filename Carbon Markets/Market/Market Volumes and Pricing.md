# Market Volumes and Pricing

Figures from CDR.fyi and Puro × Nasdaq indexes only—prices and volumes change quickly.

## CDR.fyi Biochar Carbon Removal Market Snapshot 2025

Published **2025-09-09** ([link](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/biochar-carbon-removal-market-snapshot-2025)):

- **3.04 Mt** BCR contracted from Q1 2022 through end-H1 2025
- **1.6 Mt** contracted in H1 2025 alone (driven heavily by Microsoft–Exomad Green **1.24 Mt** offtake)
- Deliveries ~**683 kt** and retirements ~**330 kt** by end-Q2 2025 (snapshot body; highlights also cite 658 kt / 302 kt—use body figures when reconciling)
- Market value of BCR (CDR.fyi 2024 pricing basis): **$14.6M (2022) → $33.9M (2023) → $181.5M (2024)**; CAGR **131.6%**
- **290** unique BCR purchasers; most common durable CDR entry by buyer count
- BCR ≈ **86%** of durable CDR *deliveries* and **92%** of *retirements* since Q1 2022; but only ~**10%** of *contracted* durable CDR (BECCS dominates contracted volume)

## CDR.fyi Q1 2026 Durable CDR Update

Published **2026-05-18** ([link](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2026-q1-durable-cdr-market-update-from-promise-to-proof)):

- **2.3 Mt** durable CDR contracted in Q1 2026 (~560% of Q1 2025)
- Biochar = **93%** of Q1 contracted volume; all top-5 deals were BCR
- Notable deals: Microsoft–Liferaft **1 Mt**; Supercritical **500 kt**; Attitude–Empacar **305 kt**; Google–AMP **200 kt**
- Deliveries **145 kt**; retirements **~100 kt** in Q1 2026

## 2024 Year in Review pricing context

Published **2025-02-14** ([link](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2024-year-in-review)):

- Overall durable CDR weighted average price **$490 → $320 / tCO₂** (2023→2024) on disclosed orders
- **Biochar was an exception:** average biochar price **increased 18%** YoY (exact $/t not disclosed in abridged public post)
- Biochar represented **86%** of 2024 delivery volume
