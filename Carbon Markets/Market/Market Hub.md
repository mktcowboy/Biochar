## How to read this folder

1. Start with **volumes** ([[Market Volumes and Pricing]]) to separate *promises* (contracted) from *proof* (delivered/retired).
2. Use **Historical Deals** for named megadeals; cross-check press releases before diligence.
3. Treat **CORCCHAR** as a *Puro* near-term transaction index—not a global BCR clearing price ([[Price Indexes and Benchmarks]] · [[Puro Pricing and CORC Indexes]]).
4. Link concentration risk to [[Risks and Controversies]] and method choice to [[Puro vs Other Standards]] · [[Verra VM0044]] · [[Isometric Biochar Protocol]] · [[CSI Global Biochar C-Sink]].
