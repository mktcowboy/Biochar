## How to use this table

- “Registry” column records what the **primary release stated**—blank means not disclosed there.

## Contextual notes on selected deals

### Microsoft × Exomad Green (1.24 Mt)

- Largest BCR agreement by volume at announcement (May 2025); among largest durable CDR deals generally (Exomad claim).
- Builds on prior 32 kt relationship.
- Biomass: forestry residues that would otherwise be open-burned; biochar to local agricultural soils.
- Facilities: Concepción & Riberalta (expanded); third facility under construction at announcement time.
- Exomad goal cited: **1 Mt CO₂ / yr by 2027**.
- Delivery share claim at announcement: **27%** of durable CDR deliveries (CDR.fyi data cited by Exomad, Apr 2025 context).

### Google × Varaha & Charm (100 kt each)

- Google called them “the largest biochar carbon removal deals to date” **as of 16 Jan 2025**—later surpassed by Microsoft–Exomad and Microsoft–Liferaft.
- Adds biochar alongside EW, DAC, Frontier, and Symbiosis toolkit.

### Google × AMP (200 kt)

- MSW organics recovered via AI sortation → biochar; initial use as **landfill daily cover**.
- Paired methane-framework work with Google.
- SPSA Virginia 20-year processing contract is the underlying project infrastructure (AMP release).

### Microsoft × Liferaft (1 Mt)

- Largest US biochar offtake announced to date (coverage consensus; Liferaft primary emphasizes rural Midwest jobs + Microsoft carbon-negative goal).
- Biochar–compost mix for approved agricultural end uses.
- Second-largest BCR purchase historically per CDR.fyi Q1 2026 (~43.5% of that quarter’s contracted durable CDR).

### Altitude × Empacar (>305 kt)

- Financier procurement of CORCs (not necessarily end-corporate retirement at signing).
- Bioflux cited as biochar program partner.
- Positions Bolivia as multi-supplier BCR hub alongside Exomad.

### Supercritical × Exomad (up to 500 kt)

- Emphasizes **near-term deliverable inventory** vs long-dated promises.
- Extends partnership; Supercritical previously facilitated **>100 kt** of Exomad credits.
- Exomad claims **>320 kt** produced to date and **>200%** YoY production growth (Apr 2026 release).

### Microsoft × Carba (44 kt)

- Storage pathway: **low-oxygen burial** / landfill cover—not classic soil amendment.
- First project on Isometric’s Biochar Storage in Low Oxygen Burial Environments module (CDR.fyi alert).
- DOE Carbon Negative Shot Pilot grant (**$7M**) referenced in Carba release.
