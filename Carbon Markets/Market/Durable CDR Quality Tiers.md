# Durable CDR Quality Tiers

Practical buyer framing for biochar and peer durable CDR—not a formal rating agency scale. Grounded in how registries and ICVCM labels differ as of **2026-09-14**. Cross-links: [[Buyers and Offtakes]] · [[Market Volumes and Pricing]] · [[Risks and Controversies]] · [[Permanence Science for Credits]].

## Why tiers matter commercially

CDR.fyi and corporate statements repeatedly cite **integrity, durability, scalability, and co-benefits** as purchase screens (e.g., Swiss Re, SEB, Nasdaq narratives in the [Biochar Snapshot 2025](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/biochar-carbon-removal-market-snapshot-2025)). Price discovery is incomplete (few disclosed $/t on megadeals), so **quality signalling** often substitutes for transparent indexes outside Puro CORCs ([[Price Indexes and Benchmarks]]).

## Practical tier table

| Tier | Typical claim | Horizon / issuance style | Biochar examples | Buyer implication |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Durable engineered CDR (high-integrity registries)** | Net atmospheric CO₂ stored; ex-post; LCA + third-party audit | Multi-century to millennial; unique serials; retirement | [[Puro CORC200+]]; [[Isometric Biochar Protocol]] 200y / 1000y; [[CSI Global Biochar C-Sink]] GPC; [[Verra VM0044]] CCP-labeled VCUs | Eligible for serious corporate neutralization portfolios; diligence still required on feedstock & delivery |
| **B — Durable but label-incomplete / transitional** | Same physics claim, weaker or pending integrity label | Same science stack; program or method awaiting CCP category approval | Puro **program** CCP-Eligible (2025-12-11) while biochar **methodology not yet CCP-Approved** ([ICVCM](https://www.icvcm.org/integrity-council-confirms-carbon-crediting-program-puro-earth-as-ccp-eligible/)); Edition 2022 CORC100+ wind-down | Some buyers accept; CCP-sensitive RFPs may prefer VM0044 until Puro biochar CCP completes |
| **C — Temporary / semi-persistent C accounting** | Time-bounded storage metrics or decay pools | ≤1000y SPC-style or short CINK products | CSI **SPC** / related temporary products (see [[CSI Global Biochar C-Sink]]) | Not interchangeable with permanent-removal claims without disclosure |
| **D — Conventional VCM offsets** | Avoidance or short-lived sequestration | Project-dependent | Non-removal forestry/RE credits sold as “offsets” | Not equivalent to BCR CDR ([[Biochar as Durable CDR]]) |
| **E — Uncertified marketing claims** | “Carbon-negative” product copy without unique retirement | No registry serial | Fertilizer / soil-product marketing without CORC/VCU/C-sink retirement | High [[Risks and Controversies]] / greenwashing risk |

## Permanence science vs commercial tiers

Commercial durability claims map to science stacks:

- **100-year inventory style:** IPCC appendix; Verra VM0044 Table 3 (H/C_org) — [[CDR Potential and IPCC Accounting]] · [[Verra VM0044]]
- **Several centuries / CORC200+:** Puro Edition 2025 power-law — [[Puro CORC200+]] · [[Puro Biochar Methodology Edition 2025]]
- **200y or 1000y (Ro path):** Isometric modules — [[Isometric Biochar Protocol]]
- **GPC >1000y vs SPC:** CSI — [[CSI Global Biochar C-Sink]]

## Delivery quality (often under-weighted)

Buyers increasingly price **near-term deliverability** alongside permanence (Supercritical/Exomad Apr 2026 narrative; CDR.fyi “promise to proof”). A Tier-A *method* with weak operational delivery underperforms a Tier-A method with proven tonnes—see delivery ratios in [[Market Volumes and Pricing]] and supplier track records in [[Supply Landscape]].

## Gaps

- No public, standardized BCR “grade” across registries.
- Limited disclosed price differentials by tier.
- dMRV platforms (e.g., Carbonfuture) appear in megadeal contracts but are not themselves a registry grade.
